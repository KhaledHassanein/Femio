## 💡 PRO TIPS FOR THE FORM

### App Name Tips:
- ✅ Include your brand first (trademark protection)
- ✅ Add 1-2 high-volume keywords
- ✅ Keep under 28 chars (better mobile display)
- ✅ Use separator: colon (:) or dash (-)
- ❌ Don't use ALL CAPS
- ❌ Don't use special characters (™, ®, ©)
- ❌ Don't use "Free", "Best", "#1" (violates policies)

### Short Description Tips:
- ✅ Lead with unique value proposition
- ✅ Include 2-3 keywords naturally
- ✅ Be specific (not generic)
- ✅ Can go slightly over 80 chars (1-3 chars OK)
- ❌ Don't keyword stuff
- ❌ Don't use clickbait
- ❌ Don't copy competitors exactly

### Category Tips:
- ✅ "Health & Fitness" is correct primary category
- ✅ "Medical" as secondary shows authority
- ❌ Don't choose "Lifestyle" (too broad)
- ❌ Don't choose "Personalization" (wrong audience)
