# Demo video script (under 3 minutes, English, YouTube)

Agent doc: the owner records it from the hackathon pack page. Screen-record the phone (Android:
quick settings → Screen record) on a phone where Settings → Assistant shows Gemini Nano "Ready".
Voice-over in English; times are targets.

| Time | Screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Today screen | "femio is a women's health app where her record never leaves her phone. Today I'll show how hybrid AI makes it more useful without breaking that promise." |
| 0:15–0:25 | Settings → Assistant | "Gemini Nano runs on this phone through Firebase AI Logic. Anything about her own body stays here." |
| 0:25–0:55 | Log → Say it in your words. Type: *"bad cramps, light bleeding and so tired"*. Tap Turn into log entries, then Add | "She writes one sentence instead of forty taps. femio's rules and Gemini Nano, on the phone, turn it into entries she confirms. The sentence is never saved or sent." |
| 0:55–1:10 | Same sheet, switch the phone to Arabic (or type *"عندي مغص جامد وصداع"*) | "It works in Arabic dialects too: rules first, the model adds to them." |
| 1:10–1:35 | Today → Visit summary | "A page for her doctor, counted from her own log. Gemini Nano puts the reviewed questions in order for her. Copy, paste, done." |
| 1:35–2:20 | Today → Ask femio. Type: *"I'm 34 and my cramps are awful, what can I do?"* Tap Ask; show the rewritten question; tap Search trusted sources | "A personal question. Before anything leaves the phone, Gemini Nano rewrites it as a general one, and she sees exactly what will be sent. Only that goes to the cloud model, grounded with Google Search. femio shows the answer only if a trusted source backs it: here, the NHS and ACOG, with every source listed." |
| 2:20–2:40 | Ask femio. Type: *"what are the safe days to avoid pregnancy?"* | "Safety rules run before any model. femio is not birth control, so this gets a fixed answer, and no model ever sees it." |
| 2:40–2:55 | Back to Today | "On-device for her data, cloud for general knowledge, rules above both. That's femio." |
