# Demo video script (under 3 minutes, English, YouTube)

Agent doc: the owner records it from the hackathon pack page. Rewritten 2026-11-24 for ADR-079 (registry
answers, Cloud boost, For you tips); the Google Search shot and the Arabic shot are gone. Screen-record the
phone (Android: quick settings → Screen record) on a phone where Settings → Assistant shows Gemini Nano
"Ready". Before recording: a profile trying to conceive for 6 to 12 months, a few days of cramps and
tiredness logged, Cloud boost off. Voice-over in English; times are targets.

| Time | Screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Today, scrolled to the For you card | "femio is a women's health app where her record never leaves her phone. Here's how hybrid AI makes it more useful without breaking that promise." |
| 0:15–0:25 | Settings → Assistant: Gemini Nano "Ready", Cloud boost "Off" | "Gemini Nano runs on this phone through Firebase AI Logic. The cloud is off unless she turns it on." |
| 0:25–0:50 | Log → Say it in your words. Type: *"bad cramps, light bleeding and so tired"*. Tap Turn into log entries, then Add | "She writes one sentence instead of forty taps. femio's rules and Gemini Nano, on the phone, turn it into entries she confirms. The sentence is never saved or sent." |
| 0:50–1:10 | Today → For you card; tap one "Source" link | "Tips chosen on her phone from her stage and what she logs. Each one is a reviewed sentence with its source. Nano only puts them in order; it can't write a tip." |
| 1:10–1:25 | Today → Visit summary | "A page for her doctor, counted from her own log, with sourced questions for her stage." |
| 1:25–2:00 | Today → Ask femio. Type: *"I'm 34 and my cramps are awful, what can I do?"* Tap Ask. Scroll to Sources | "A personal question, so it stays on the phone. femio finds passages in a library of trusted sources it carries, here MedlinePlus, and Gemini Nano summarises them with numbered citations. No source, no answer." |
| 2:00–2:15 | Ask another question: *"heavy bleeding and dizzy"* | "Safety rules run before any model. This shows ACOG's emergency guidance and the help page." |
| 2:15–2:40 | Settings → Assistant → tap the Cloud boost switch; show the consent screen; tap Not now | "On a phone without Nano she can choose Cloud boost. This screen shows exactly what could be sent: four broad facts and general questions she approves. Never her notes or dates." |
| 2:40–2:55 | Back to Today | "On-device for her data, sourced answers, rules above both. In English, Arabic, French and Spanish. That's femio." |
