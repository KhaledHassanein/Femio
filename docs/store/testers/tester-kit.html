2. The 14-day test plan

Each task is about 5 minutes. Use invented data only. On days with no task, just keep femio installed. The wording is written so the owner can send it to testers as it stands.

1
Install and first look
Install femio from the opt-in link. Open it and go through onboarding (use any invented year of birth and cycle length). Read the privacy notice once.Look for: Onboarding clear? Any word you did not understand? Did the privacy note match what you expected?
2
Log one day
Open the Log screen and record one invented day (flow, a symptom, a note). Go back to Today.Look for: Easy to find? Did it save? Anything confusing about the options?
3
Say it
On the Log screen try "Say it in your words": type a sentence such as "light bleeding and a headache today" and check the entries it proposes before you confirm.Look for: Did it understand? Did it propose anything you did not say? Did it ask you to confirm before saving?
4
Rest day
Nothing to do. Keep femio installed.
5
Calendar
Open the calendar. Look at the period range and the fertile window. Tap a few days.Look for: Do ranges make sense? Does anything look like a single certain date, or feel too sure? Any wrong word?
6
Learn and Help now
Open Learn and read one article that interests you. Open Help now once to see what it shows (do not call any number).Look for: Easy to read? Anything that sounds like a diagnosis or a treatment instruction?
7
Ask femio
Open Ask femio from Today or Learn. Ask a general question (for example about ovulation or early pregnancy). Check that it shows its sources. Then try a question it probably cannot answer.Look for: Are sources shown? Did it say "not covered yet" when it could not answer? Did anything sound medical or unsafe?
8
Rest day
Nothing to do. Keep femio installed.
9
Visit summary
From Today, open the visit summary (a page for a doctor built from your log). Read it through.Look for: Is it clear? Does it contain anything you did not log? Does it read like advice or like a plain summary?
10
Backup and restore
Settings: save a backup file (choose a passphrase you can remember and a folder). Then, only if you are comfortable, try deleting everything and restoring from the backup.Look for: Did every step make sense? Was the passphrase warning clear? Did the restore bring everything back?
11
Settings and lock
In Settings look at every option once: PIN lock, reminders, Hijri dates, Assistant. Set a PIN, close femio, reopen it. Leave Cloud boost off unless you want to read its consent screen (do not enable it with anything personal).Look for: Any setting you did not understand? Did the lock work? Did the Assistant page explain what runs where?
12
Arabic
Switch the language to Arabic in Settings. Visit Today, Log, the calendar and one article. Switch back if you wish.Look for: Wrong or odd words? Anything cut off, overlapping or in the wrong direction? Are numbers shown in Western digits (0-9)? Is the name "femio" written in Latin letters?
13
Dark mode
Set your phone to dark mode (in the phone's settings) and open femio. Look at Today, the calendar and an article.Look for: Anything hard to read? Any bright patch or wrong colour?
14
Last day and form
Open femio once more. Keep it installed through the end of today. Then fill in the feedback form.Look for: Anything you want to add that the form did not ask?

What to look for, on every day

  • Wrong, odd or untranslated words (in any language).
  • Crashes, freezes, blank screens or buttons that do nothing.
  • Confusing screens or steps you could not finish.
  • Anything that felt unsafe: advice that sounded like a diagnosis, a treatment instruction, or a promise about pregnancy or avoiding pregnancy.
  • Anything that sounded medical or too certain (femio should give ranges and say it is not a medical device).
  • Anything that seemed to ask for or send personal information.
