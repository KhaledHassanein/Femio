# femio closed-test tester kit

Agent-facing source. The owner uses `tester-kit.html` (same content, with copy buttons). Placeholders: `{GROUP_LINK}` (Google Group join link), `{OPT_IN_LINK}` (Play closed-test opt-in link), `{FORM_LINK}` (Google Form).

Scope of claims: only what the 1.0.0 build does per `docs/CHANGELOG.md` (private on-device records, no account, onboarding, Log, Say it, calendar with ranges, Learn, Help now, Ask femio with cited sources, visit summary, backup file, PIN lock, Settings with language / Hijri / reminders / Assistant, four UI languages). The wordmark is always lowercase femio. Never pitch it as Arabic-first. No Ramadan content. Wellness only. Dates are deliberately absent: the owner starts the clock when the 12th tester opts in.

## 2. The 14-day test plan

Each task is about 5 minutes. Use invented data only. On days with no task, just keep femio installed. The wording is written so the owner can send it to testers as it stands.

| Day | Task | What to do | Look for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Install and first look | Install femio from the opt-in link. Open it and go through onboarding (use any invented year of birth and cycle length). Read the privacy notice once. | Onboarding clear? Any word you did not understand? Did the privacy note match what you expected? |
| 2 | Log one day | Open the Log screen and record one invented day (flow, a symptom, a note). Go back to Today. | Easy to find? Did it save? Anything confusing about the options? |
| 3 | Say it | On the Log screen try "Say it in your words": type a sentence such as "light bleeding and a headache today" and check the entries it proposes before you confirm. | Did it understand? Did it propose anything you did not say? Did it ask you to confirm before saving? |
| 4 | Rest day | Nothing to do. Keep femio installed. | - |
| 5 | Calendar | Open the calendar. Look at the period range and the fertile window. Tap a few days. | Do ranges make sense? Does anything look like a single certain date, or feel too sure? Any wrong word? |
| 6 | Learn and Help now | Open Learn and read one article that interests you. Open Help now once to see what it shows (do not call any number). | Easy to read? Anything that sounds like a diagnosis or a treatment instruction? |
| 7 | Ask femio | Open Ask femio from Today or Learn. Ask a general question (for example about ovulation or early pregnancy). Check that it shows its sources. Then try a question it probably cannot answer. | Are sources shown? Did it say "not covered yet" when it could not answer? Did anything sound medical or unsafe? |
| 8 | Rest day | Nothing to do. Keep femio installed. | - |
| 9 | Visit summary | From Today, open the visit summary (a page for a doctor built from your log). Read it through. | Is it clear? Does it contain anything you did not log? Does it read like advice or like a plain summary? |
| 10 | Backup and restore | Settings: save a backup file (choose a passphrase you can remember and a folder). Then, only if you are comfortable, try deleting everything and restoring from the backup. | Did every step make sense? Was the passphrase warning clear? Did the restore bring everything back? |
| 11 | Settings and lock | In Settings look at every option once: PIN lock, reminders, Hijri dates, Assistant. Set a PIN, close femio, reopen it. Leave Cloud boost off unless you want to read its consent screen (do not enable it with anything personal). | Any setting you did not understand? Did the lock work? Did the Assistant page explain what runs where? |
| 12 | Arabic | Switch the language to Arabic in Settings. Visit Today, Log, the calendar and one article. Switch back if you wish. | Wrong or odd words? Anything cut off, overlapping or in the wrong direction? Are numbers shown in Western digits (0-9)? Is the name "femio" written in Latin letters? |
| 13 | Dark mode | Set your phone to dark mode (in the phone's settings) and open femio. Look at Today, the calendar and an article. | Anything hard to read? Any bright patch or wrong colour? |
| 14 | Last day and form | Open femio once more. Keep it installed through the end of today. Then fill in the feedback form. | Anything you want to add that the form did not ask? |

### What to look for, on every day

- Wrong, odd or untranslated words (in any language).
- Crashes, freezes, blank screens or buttons that do nothing.
- Confusing screens or steps you could not finish.
- Anything that felt unsafe: advice that sounded like a diagnosis, a treatment instruction, or a promise about pregnancy or avoiding pregnancy.
- Anything that sounded medical or too certain (femio should give ranges and say it is not a medical device).
- Anything that seemed to ask for or send personal information.

## 3. Feedback form (for the Google Form)

### Before you start (show at the top of the form)

- Please do NOT type your own health details anywhere in this form, and do NOT attach screenshots that show personal data (names, dates of your own cycle, notes). Describe what you saw in words instead. This form does not ask for your name or email.

### About your phone

- 1. Phone make and model (for example Samsung A14). [short text, required]
- 2. Android version (Settings > About phone). [short text, required]
- 3. Screen size: small / medium / large / tablet. [multiple choice]
- 4. Is your phone in dark mode most of the time? [yes / no / sometimes]

### How you used femio

- 5. Which language did you mainly use? [English / Arabic / French / Spanish / more than one]
- 6. Did you switch to Arabic at some point? [yes / no / it was already Arabic]
- 7. Which parts did you try? [checkboxes: Onboarding, Log, Say it, Calendar, Learn, Help now, Ask femio, Visit summary, Backup and restore, PIN lock, Settings, Reminders]
- 8. Roughly how many times did you open femio over the 14 days? [0-2 / 3-5 / 6-10 / more than 10]

### Ratings (1 = very poor, 5 = very good)

- 9. How easy was it to start (install and onboarding)? [1-5]
- 10. How easy was it to find and use the main features? [1-5]
- 11. How clear were the words and explanations? [1-5]
- 12. How well did the screens look and work in your language? [1-5]
- 13. How well did femio explain that your records stay on your phone? [1-5]
- 14. How helpful were the answers from Ask femio, and were the sources clear? [1-5, or not tried]
- 15. How likely are you to recommend femio to a friend? [1-5]

### Problems

- 16. Did the app crash, freeze or show a blank screen? [no / once / more than once] If yes, which screen and what were you doing? [free text]
- 17. Did you see a wrong, odd or untranslated word? Which screen, and what did it say? [free text]
- 18. Was any screen or step confusing? [free text]
- 19. Did anything feel unsafe, or sound medical (like a diagnosis, a treatment instruction or a promise)? Which screen, and what did it say? [free text]
- 20. Did anything look wrong in dark mode or in Arabic (cut off, overlapping, wrong direction)? [free text]

### Anything else

- 21. What did you like most? [free text]
- 22. What is the one thing you would change first? [free text]
- 23. Anything else you would like to tell us? Remember: no personal health details. [free text]
- 24. Did you keep femio installed for all 14 days? [yes / no, I removed it on day __]

## 5. How the owner tracks the 14 days

- [ ] Before sending anything: in Play Console create the closed track "femio closed", upload the .aab, add a Google Group (femio-testers) as the tester list, and copy the opt-in link and the group link into the {OPT_IN_LINK} and {GROUP_LINK} placeholders. Create the Google Form from section 3 (turn off "Collect email addresses") and put its link into {FORM_LINK}. Replace the placeholders before copying a message.
- [ ] Recruit 15 to 20 people, not 12. Some will not follow through, and a margin protects the 14-day count.
- [ ] Day 0: when the 12th tester has opted in (Play Console > Testing > Closed testing > Testers shows the number opted in), write today's date. That is the start of the clock. Record it in docs/33_REBUILD_V3/PROGRESS.md R5.4.
- [ ] Every day: look at the tester count. It must stay at 12 or more opted in. The 14 days must be in a row.
- [ ] Day 3, day 7, day 13: send the reminders from section 4.
- [ ] If the count drops below 12 (someone leaves the group or opts out): add testers at once and watch the count. Google's rule is 12 testers opted in for 14 continuous days, so if the count fell under 12, the safe reading is that the clock restarts the day you are back at 12 or more (the release runbook says the same). Confirm the exact rule on the Play Console production-access page before relying on it [NEEDS CHECK].
- [ ] If you upload a fixed build during the window, put it on the same track. Testers receive it automatically and stay opted in. Do not create a new track.
- [ ] Day 14: confirm in Play Console that the count has been 12 or more for 14 days, then apply for production access in the same place. Google asks a few questions about the test (how you recruited testers, what feedback you received); keep the form answers as your evidence.
- [ ] Read the form answers. Any answer under question 19 (unsafe or medical-sounding) is a stop-and-review item: send it to the lead session before production.
- [ ] Never paste testers' names or emails into git or chat. Keep the tester list in Play Console and the Google Group only.
