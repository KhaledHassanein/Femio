# Changelog
All notable changes to Femio V2.

## [Unreleased]
### Fixed
- Critical client-side hydration crash (all signed-in pages): `env.ts` validated the whole `process.env`, which Next.js leaves empty in the client bundle, so validation threw and `/welcome`, `/track`, `/dashboard`, and `/settings` crashed on hydration in production (SSR/curl couldn't detect it). Now references each `NEXT_PUBLIC_*` var explicitly. Caught by the new Playwright e2e harness and verified fixed on live prod.

### Added
- Author & medical-reviewer bio pages (P1.9b.3b, ADR-016, → apps/cms/src/collections/People.ts, apps/web/src/app/[locale]/(public)/authors/): a **People** collection in the CMS holds bios — name, credentials, job title, affiliation, a short biography, and links to professional profiles — and the website publishes them at `/authors` and `/authors/{name}`, with `Person` structured data so search engines can see who writes and who approves Femio's health content (an E-E-A-T requirement in health). Bios are public only when published; until the first ones exist the index shows a friendly "bios appear here as our first guides are published" state. Linked from the footer, cross-linked to the editorial-policy and medical-review pages, in the sitemap, all four languages. Needs a one-time CMS schema push (runbook C3.6); article bylines linking to these pages land with the first P1.9 articles.
- Privacy policy + Terms of use pages, published as clearly-marked **drafts** (P1.9b.3, ADR-015, → apps/web/src/app/[locale]/(public)/{privacy,terms}/): plain-language pages explaining what Femio collects and why, who else can see it (nobody but our database/hosting provider — never advertisers or third-party analytics), where it lives (EU), how long we keep it, your export/delete rights, anonymous mode, teen safeguards, and security — plus the terms, including the wellness boundary (Femio informs and tracks; it doesn't give medical advice, and fertile-window dates are not a reliable way to avoid pregnancy). Both carry a banner saying the text is a draft while our lawyers and data-protection officer finalise the binding version, and both are deliberately kept **out of search and the sitemap** until then, so a draft is never mistaken for our policy of record. Linked from the footer and cross-linked; all four languages, accessibility-checked in LTR and RTL.
- Content-first homepage + full content library + global nav (P1.9b / P1.8, → apps/web/src/app/[locale]/(public)/): the placeholder home is now a real landing page — a hero with the product promise and a card grid into the public library (Learn, Conditions, Calculators, FAQ, Glossary), with the tracker app as a portal you enter from it. Added **Conditions** guides, an **FAQ** page (with FAQ rich-results markup) and a **Glossary** (with glossary markup), all read from the CMS and reachable without an account. A **persistent site header and footer** now wrap every public page so you can move around the whole library from anywhere. Added **About**, **Editorial policy**, and **Medical review** trust pages (linked in the footer) that explain our sourcing and clinician-review standards. Full i18n; accessibility-checked.

### Fixed
- Array-valued UI messages (e.g. a page's list of points) were being collapsed into objects by the translation DB-overlay once any approved `ui_strings` existed, which would break pages that render those lists. The overlay now reconstructs arrays correctly (covered by a test).
- Public content pages from the CMS (P1.6.2 / P1.11.1, → apps/web/src/lib/cms/, app/[locale]/learn/): the web app now reads **published** articles from the Payload CMS and renders them at a `/learn` hub and `/learn/[slug]` pages — with the rich-text body, cited sources, a context-appropriate wellness disclaimer, a "medically reviewed" mark, and MedicalWebPage/Breadcrumb structured data; published articles are added to the sitemap. It degrades gracefully: if the CMS isn't configured or is unreachable, the pages show a friendly empty state and the site still builds. Full i18n. (Activate by pointing `NEXT_PUBLIC_CMS_URL` at the CMS and publishing an article.)
- Sign-in for returning users — password, magic link, and Google (P1.12.1, → apps/web/src/app/[locale]/sign-in/, reset-password/, app/api/auth/callback/): returning users (and staff who need the translation portal) can sign in with an **email + password**, a **passwordless magic link**, or **Google** (shown once you configure the provider). Forgot-password sends a reset link to a set-a-new-password page, and Settings gained a card to set/change a password (handy for accounts created passwordlessly). A callback route establishes the session and returns you to the dashboard. Full i18n.
- Bulk CSV export/import for the translation portal (P1.19.2, → apps/web/src/lib/i18n/csv.ts, components/admin/translation-editor.tsx): translators can **Export CSV** for a language (key, English master, current translation), edit all of it offline in Excel, and **Import CSV** to bulk-save — with a toggle to approve the imported rows (publish) or keep them as drafts. Unknown keys are ignored and empty cells skipped. The file uses UTF-8 so Arabic and accents open correctly in Excel.
- Translation portal + DB-backed UI strings (P1.19, ADR-014, → apps/web/src/lib/i18n/, components/admin/translation-editor.tsx, app/[locale]/(app)/admin/translations/): a staff-only portal (admin/translator) to review and approve interface strings per language, with a per-locale status board, filter, and one-click Save & approve that publishes the string immediately. Under the hood the local JSON catalogs stay the base and only **human-approved** rows from the database are overlaid on top, so nothing changes until a string is deliberately approved — an empty or unavailable table simply leaves the app on JSON (no regression). A seed script imports the JSON catalogs into the database (English pre-approved as master; Arabic/French/Spanish as drafts awaiting review). Activation needs a one-time staff-role grant + running the seed with a service key. Machine-draft prefill (DeepL) and live preview are tracked as a follow-up.
- Doctor-visit summary / PDF export (P1.16.1, → apps/web/src/lib/account/visit-summary.ts, app/[locale]/(app)/summary/): a printable `/summary` page (linked from Settings) presents a curated, human-readable snapshot — cycle count/average/range and recent periods, recent symptoms with intensity, medications, self-reported flagged topics, and key dates — for sharing with a clinician. PDF is produced by the browser's own print / Save-as-PDF, so health data never leaves your device or session (no PDF library, no server rendering); app chrome is hidden when printing. It carries a clear "personal tracking summary, not a medical record or clinical advice" disclaimer. Built on a pure, unit-tested summary builder. Full i18n.
- Per-symptom severity + custom symptoms (P1.14.1, → apps/web/src/lib/cycle/symptoms.ts, components/cycle/log-symptoms.tsx): symptom logging now records an intensity **per symptom** rather than one shared value, and lets people **add their own** symptoms (normalized + de-duplicated, stored on the open symptom type and shown as typed). The curated list grew from 16 to 26 (mood swings, low motivation, joint pain, constipation, diarrhoea, hot flushes, spotting, brain fog, higher/lower sex drive) in all four locales. The full 100+ taxonomy stays a translation-portal (CMS) task. Backed by pure helpers with unit tests and an end-to-end check.
- Fertile window + ovulation on the tracker calendar (P1.13.2, → apps/web/src/components/cycle/cycle-calendar.tsx): the month calendar now overlays the estimated fertile window (soft teal band) and ovulation day (highlighted), derived from the last period and average cycle, with a legend, screen-reader labels, and a clear conception-awareness-only note (never framed as contraception). The predicted-period band stays amber so the phases read as distinct.
- Cycle calendar + flow intensity (P1.13.1, → apps/web/src/lib/cycle/calendar.ts, components/cycle/cycle-calendar.tsx): the tracker now shows a real month calendar — an accessible table with previous/next navigation and locale-aware month/weekday labels — where logged period days are marked (with a dot whose shade reflects flow, and the flow named in each cell's screen-reader label so it never depends on colour alone), the predicted next-period window shows as a soft band, and today is ringed. Logging a period now offers a flow choice (spotting/light/medium/heavy) saved with the entry. Arabic weeks start on Saturday; RTL verified. Built on a pure, tested month-grid helper.
- Role-based access control foundation (P1.18, → docs/18_RBAC_MODEL.md, apps/web/src/lib/auth/): the web app gained role-awareness on top of the existing DB + CMS role model — `STAFF_ROLES`, pure `hasRole`/`isStaff` predicates, `getMyRoles()` (RLS-scoped, empty for consumers), and a `requireStaff()` Server-Component guard ready for the admin-portal pages (P1.19–P1.21). The RLS audit now also proves **no privilege escalation** (a non-admin cannot grant itself a role or read another user's roles). The five-role model (admin/editor/medical_reviewer/translator/support) and where each layer enforces it is documented; `support` is intentionally app-side only (no Payload CMS access).
- Security review + automated RLS audit (P1.23, → docs/compliance/SECURITY_REVIEW.md, scripts/security/rls-audit.mjs): `pnpm rls-audit` proves the data-layer guarantees against the live project using only the publishable key — the consent gate (health writes blocked pre-consent, allowed after), owner-only isolation (a second user cannot read/update/delete another's rows), the unauthenticated anon role reading no health data, and the GDPR delete cascade (10/10 pass). A SECURITY_REVIEW doc maps Femio's posture to the OWASP Top-10 (2021), documents the rate-limiting/abuse posture, and lists the remaining launch-gate items (independent pen-test, at-rest-encryption confirmation, `pnpm audit` in CI, CSP/headers, staff MFA, breach alerting).
- Accessibility audit in CI + DB-free e2e (P1.22 / P1.5.2, → apps/web/e2e/a11y.spec.ts, ci.yml): a Playwright + axe-core WCAG 2.1 A/AA scan (serious+critical gate) over home, calculators, help-now, welcome and the open crisis dialog — in both an LTR and an RTL locale — plus the crisis and home e2e now run in CI on every PR (built app served via `next start`). ### Added
- Consent-gated, health-field-free analytics foundation (P1.20, ADR-017, → apps/web/src/lib/analytics/, scripts/analytics-lint.mjs, docs/19_ANALYTICS_EVENT_SCHEMA.md): the groundwork for product analytics that **cannot** carry health data. Rather than trusting careful coding, the event catalogue is a *closed* list — fixed events, fixed properties, and every value drawn from a short fixed set, with no free-form text, numbers or dates anywhere in it — so an article slug, a symptom name, a cycle date or a lab value has nowhere to travel. A runtime check drops anything that deviates, and a new CI guard (`pnpm analytics-lint`) fails the build if the schema later drifts, if session recording or autocapture is switched on, or if anything tries to measure the crisis-help surface. Analytics is **opt-in** under its own new consent purpose and is off for everyone until the toggle ships; it is not bundled into the existing "show me my patterns" consent, which is about your own data being shown back to you. If the analytics service is ever connected it must be its EU region — a non-EU address fails the build rather than quietly sending data abroad. **Nothing is sent today**: with no analytics service configured, no third-party script loads at all.
