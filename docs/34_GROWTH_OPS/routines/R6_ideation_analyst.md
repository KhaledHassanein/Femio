# R6 · Ideation analyst (Sonnet, `femio-competitor-analyst`, monthly, first Friday 07:00 Cairo)

## Read first
`CLAUDE.md` (the six product rules and ADR-070/072/075), `docs/STATE.md`, the wedge
sentence, `docs/32_STUDY/00_SYNTHESIS.md` §"what femio is not", the last two monthly reports,
`docs/34_GROWTH_OPS/02_MONETIZATION.md` §2.4 (the free/Plus matrix), and
`reports/feedback/YYYY-MM.md` if the owner has pasted the month's answers.

## Inputs
- Competitors' store "What's new" and release notes (Flo, Clue, Natural Cycles, Ovia,
  Stardust, Premom, Glow, Hawaa, Al Malika, roony+), read on the web.
- Play reviews (Egypt and Saudi storefronts) of femio and of the two nearest Arabic apps:
  the 50 most recent each, read for recurring asks.
- r/TTC, r/PCOS, r/BabyBumps and عالم حواء: threads from the month that name a need.
- The one-question feedback answers (owner's file; it must contain no health detail; if it
  does, stop and tell the lead).

## Steps
1. List candidate ideas with their evidence (a quote or a count, with link and date).
2. Score each: evidence (1–3) × fit with the wedge (1–3); cost class S/M/L; the product rule
   it touches (1–6); the tier it belongs to (free / Plus); whether it needs a server, an
   account or health data leaving the phone (if yes: section "Rejected by rule 4", never
   promoted).
3. Rank the top 10. For each: one paragraph, the evidence, what the smallest version is.
4. "Stop doing": three observations about femio that the evidence argues against.
5. Add a `[ ]` line to `docs/33_REBUILD_V3/PROGRESS.md` for any idea the lead accepts
   (the lead does this; the routine lists the lines ready to paste).

## Output
`reports/monthly/YYYY-MM-ideas.md`: *Summary*, *Top 10*, *Stop doing*, *Rejected by rule 4*,
*Sources*. Under 2,000 words.

## Rubric (lead)
Every idea has dated evidence; nothing promoted violates rules 1–6; cost classes are
plausible against the codebase; the file names no person.
