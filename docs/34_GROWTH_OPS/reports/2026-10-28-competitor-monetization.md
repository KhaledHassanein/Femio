## 3. Recommendation

### (i) Keep free (the YMYL funnel)
1. **All 100 articles, 5 hubs, 7 calculators, help-now, pregnancy warning signs.** Rule 2 requires every article and tool stay reachable. Clue's article page has no paywall (S12); BabyCenter is fully free (S21). Avoid Pregnancy+'s pattern of gating a contraction timer behind premium or ad-watching (S23).
2. **Core logging, range predictions, Hijri dates, PIN lock, backup file.** Privacy and safety are the brand.

### (ii) Premium set
Premium must be **additive** (new depth, nothing removed). Billing is a **deferred item** (`00_PLAN.md` line 70; ledger C-08 unresolved), so it needs an owner decision and an ADR.

| Rank | Feature | Proven by | Femio today | Effort | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Visit report: PDF of her own logs for a clinician | Flo doctor's report (S2), Premom reports (S18), Balance consultation examples (S24) | Logs exist, no report | M | Rule 1 ("discuss with your clinician"); Rule 4 (built on the phone, shared by her) |
| 2 | Pattern insights with ranges and confidence | Clue Plus, Glow, Flo (S8, S19, S2) | Range predictions exist | M | Rule 3 (no late counter, "no confident window") |
| 3 | TTC and PCOS programme, offline, Arabic | Clue Conceive (S13); Flo checker (S2) | Guides exist | L | Rules 1 and 2. Flo withdrew its checker in UK/EU (S2): no screener scores |
| 4 | Pregnancy extras: birth plan, hospital-bag checklist, extra weekly content | Pregnancy+ (S23) | Pregnancy tools exist | S to M | Rule 1; warning signs, kick counter and contraction timer stay free |
| 5 | Expert-reviewed audio or video courses | Flo, Balance+ (S2, S24) | None | L | Medical review per `femio-medical-content` |

Not available to Femio: partner sharing, chat, community, cloud sync (deferred). **"Removes ads" cannot be a premium benefit as designed.** The app has no ads and no network permission (`03_LEGAL_PACK.md` lines 66, 77), and with no accounts the website cannot know who subscribed. Only ad-funded Arabic apps sell it (S26, S27).

### (iii) Price check, 0.99 USD per month and 9.99 USD per year
- Conversion: 0.99 USD is about 55.5 EGP at 56.09 EGP per USD (S35, secondary) and about 3.71 SAR at a 3.75 peg (peg not verified here). 9.99 USD is about 560 EGP and 37.5 SAR.
- Floors: Google's 2015 minimums were E£2 and 0.99 SAR (S33); in 2021 Egypt and Saudi Arabia were listed as sub-dollar markets (S34). **Current exact floors: not verified** (the table did not load); check Play Console, Subscriptions, base plan prices. Both prices are far above the 2015 floors.
- Fees: markets outside Australia, EEA, Japan, UK and US pay 15% on auto-renewing subscriptions (S31), so 9.99 USD nets about 8.49 USD before tax (my arithmetic; tax handling not verified).
- Market: Clue $39.99 per year, Pregnancy+ $34.99, Balance+ $49.99 (S8, S23, S24); Al Malika SAR 104.99 (about 28 USD), roony+ SAR 199.99 (about 53 USD) (S27, S28). Only GP Apps is near 9.99 per year, and its Egypt price is EGP 199.99, about 3.6 USD (S20, S35). The 0.99 monthly is a fifth to a seventh of Hawaa, Al Malika and roony's monthly prices.
- Reading: defensible for Egypt (price index 41 against 100 for the US, S35), low for Saudi Arabia, and the annual saves only 16% against twelve months. **Hypothesis, not finding:** set country prices by hand in Play Console (higher for Saudi Arabia) and test an annual trial. The Stardust study shows trial and price presentation moved conversion, in relative terms only (S17b).

### (iv) Ad pattern and AdSense constraints (website only)
**Competitor unit counts and positions: not verified.** BabyCenter and What to Expect could not be fetched; Clue's article page showed none in text (S12), which a text read cannot prove. The pattern below comes from policy limits and is a hypothesis:
- **Article pages: at most 2 units.** One responsive unit after the second section, below the first screen; one after the sources. **Zero** on hubs, `/learn` index lists, calculators, help-now, warning signs, legal and privacy pages. Ads in navigation placements are prohibited (S36).
- **Zero on condition hubs and on PCOS, fertility, pregnancy-loss and mental-health articles at launch.** The ad request exposes the page topic.
- **Format:** label "Advertisement" or "إعلان" (allowed, S36); no pop-ups, interstitials, floating or sticky ads (S36, S42); ad density under 30% on mobile (S42). Google has allowed 300x250 above the fold on mobile since 2017 (S43).
- **Health constraints:**
  - No health-specific ban in the policies I read; claims against expert consensus are prohibited (S39).
  - "Birth control", "Sexual and reproductive health", "Drugs & supplements", "Weight loss", "Cosmetic procedures", "Religion", "Astrology" and "Dating" ads are **allowed by default; the publisher must block them** in Brand safety, and blocking is not guaranteed (S37). Blocking "Birth control" protects Rule 1.
  - Sexual content, incl. "sexual performance advice", is a restricted label with reduced or no ads (S38).
  - Personalised ads in the EEA, UK and Switzerland need a Google-certified consent tool (S41). Use non-personalised ads everywhere.
  - Applicants must be 18+ with original content (S40). AdSense payout method for Egypt: not verified.

### (v) Three risks
1. **Trust.** Ads contradict current statements: "no ads", "no analytics and no tracking cookies", Play "No ads" (`03_LEGAL_PACK.md` lines 66, 88, 235) and Rule 4 ("the website collects nothing"). Period apps lost trust over ad and analytics SDKs: the FTC found Flo shared health data with Facebook and Google analytics (S44); Premom paid a $100,000 penalty plus $100,000 to three states and is barred from sharing health data for advertising (S18, FTC page); a jury found Meta liable over Flo app data in Aug 2025 (secondary, S45). Mitigation: site only, non-personalised, none on health pages, none in the app, rewritten privacy notice. This needs an ADR and an owner decision.
2. **Store policy.** Play Billing is required for in-app unlocks, and apps may not lead users to another payment method (S32, sections 2 and 4). **PayPal and Paddle can serve site-only products, never the app premium.** Alternative-billing eligibility for Egypt or Saudi Arabia: not verified. The release gate fails any manifest requesting `INTERNET` (`05_RELEASE_RUNBOOK.md` line 15); whether Play Billing works without it is not verified (community reports only), so test on a real device first.
3. **Legal.** Health data is sensitive under Saudi PDPL and Egypt Law 151 of 2020, with explicit-consent duties (secondary summaries, S46; statute text not retrieved). Ad cookies on health-topic pages and any paid "screener" raise exposure. Have a qualified lawyer review before ads or billing ship.

### One small test (under 1 hour, owner)
Open BabyCenter, What to Expect, Flo and Clue article pages on a phone, count ad units and positions, and screenshot. This turns section (iv) into evidence.
