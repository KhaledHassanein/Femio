# Canva templates (G3 / R6.7)

Built by a session through the Canva connector once the owner has created the Canva
account (owner guide part F1). Until then every row is `pending` and R5 writes
`design: pending` in its post files.

| Key | Size | Layers | Canva link |
|---|---|---|---|
| `femio / square` | 1080×1080 | plum field, headline AR, headline EN, one-line answer, wordmark, pictogram slot | pending |
| `femio / story` | 1080×1920 | the same, vertical, safe zones for platform UI | pending |
| `femio / channel` | 1080×1350 | large Arabic headline, QR to the page | pending |
| `femio / clinic-a6` | A6 print, 300 dpi | bilingual line "سجلاتكِ لا تغادر هاتفكِ", QR to `/ar/app?ref=clinic-<n>` | pending |
| `femio / figure` | 1080×1080 | one sourced figure, Dusk Bloom chart tokens, source line | pending |
| `femio / og` | 1200×630 | generated by the site per article; not a Canva template | n/a |

Design rules: Dusk Bloom tokens only (`assets/brand/tokens/femio.tokens.css`); no photos of
people; no rings, no single date, no "late" counter; the wordmark is always the Latin
lowercase **femio**; Arabic text in IBM Plex Sans Arabic or Noto Naskh Arabic, Latin in Plus
Jakarta Sans or Newsreader.
