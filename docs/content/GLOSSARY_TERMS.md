# Femio medical terminology glossary

This glossary fixes the preferred medical terminology used across Femio's English, Arabic,
French, and Spanish content. Definitions are written for readers and may be published as
plain-language glossary copy. Arabic uses conservative Modern Standard Arabic and addresses
the reader without colloquial or intimate wording. Abbreviations may follow the full term on
first use, but they do not replace it.

## Usage rules

Use the preferred term above whenever the English concept is the same. A common-language alias
may appear once in parentheses when it helps comprehension, but it must not replace the medical
term later in the same article. Do not translate acronyms letter by letter: write the full
localized term first, followed by the established acronym shown above.

Definitions describe concepts; they do not establish that any concept applies to a reader. In
articles, retain the English source's uncertainty and care-routing language around every term.
