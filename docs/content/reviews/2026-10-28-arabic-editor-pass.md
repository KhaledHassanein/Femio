# Arabic editor pass: 11 Arabic guides vs English sources (2026-10-28)

**What this is.** A read-only editorial review of the 11 `*.ar.md` guides in `docs/content/articles/`, each compared line by line with its English source. No article was edited. The lead applies the changes below. The reviewer is an AI (Claude Sonnet 5.5), **not** a native clinician sign-off: every `medically_reviewed_by` field stays `PENDING`.

**Glossary used:** `docs/content/GLOSSARY_TERMS.md` (cited as "glossary"). It has no entries for screening, hypoglycaemia, hCG, insemination or chaperone, and no rule for "period" versus "cycle" in everyday Arabic. Those are listed as glossary gaps.

**How to read the counts.** Each proposed change in a per-guide table is tagged at the start of its last column: **F** = fidelity (the Arabic differs from the English in content), **R** = register or readability, **H** = house rule (glossary term, wordmark, digits, dashes, Hijri, front matter). Set-wide items that repeat in every guide are listed once under "Patterns across the set" and are **not** in the per-guide counts.

**Checked and clean in all 11 guides** (scripted search plus reading): the wordmark is always Latin `femio` (0 uses of فيميو, 0 capital "Femio" in running Arabic text); 0 Arabic-Indic digits (all digits are 0-9); 0 em or en dashes in the Arabic body; 0 uses of "يرجى"; 0 "من المهم أن" chains. Hijri: none of the English sources mentions Hijri or a Hijri-style date, and none of the Arabic files adds one (no change needed). The only date-like mention is the Ramadan guide's editor note that the Ramadan 2027 date is deliberately left out of the body.

## Patterns across the set

1. **Intercourse wording is split three ways.** "الجماع" x8 (irregular-cycles x6, when-to-seek-fertility-help x2), "العلاقة الحميمة" x14 (fertile-window, trying-to-conceive, when-to-seek-fertility-help, when-to-take-pregnancy-test), "العلاقة الزوجية" x1. The brief asks for "العلاقة الزوجية". Note "الزوجية" assumes marriage, while the English also covers insemination and clinic pathways, so the lead should pick one phrase once and apply it everywhere. "الجماع" must go in any case.
2. **Inclusion content was dropped silently.** The English mentions donor sperm, same-sex relationships, parenting alone and home insemination. The Arabic drops these in trying-to-conceive-guide, when-to-seek-fertility-help and fertile-window-explained (and turns "partner" into "زوج" once). For Egypt and Saudi readers this may be right, but no ADR records it. Record the decision in `docs/DECISIONS.md` or restore the text.
3. **Glossary drift, and glossary gaps.** Used instead of the glossary term: تكيّس المبايض (x12, glossary: المبيض المتعدد الكيسات), بطانة الرحم المهاجرة (x5, glossary: الانتباذ البطاني الرحمي), تسمم الحمل (x4, glossary: مقدمات الارتعاج), الهرمون الملوتن, درجة حرارة الجسم القاعدية (x2, glossary: الأساسية), موعد الولادة (x4), أطفال الأنابيب, تشنج/انقباضات, التبقيع. Lay aliases are allowed once in brackets by the glossary's own rule. Add glossary entries for: screening (الفحص الاستقصائي or فحوص الكشف, pick one), hypoglycaemia (هبوط السكر), hCG (هرمون الحمل), insemination, chaperone.
4. **The doctor heading is masculine in all 11 guides** ("متى تراجعين الطبيب؟") and the red-flag boxes are single sentences of 36 to 52 words. The heading text is hard-coded in `scripts/content-lint.mjs` line 49 (`ar: { doctor: "متى تراجعين الطبيب؟" ... }`), so change both together. Proposed heading: `متى تطلبين المشورة الطبية؟`. For the boxes, use the bullet layout the Ramadan guide already uses.
5. **femio takes both genders as a subject.** Masculine in fertile-window, trying-to-conceive and when-to-take-pregnancy-test ("لا يستطيع femio"); feminine in irregular-cycles, pregnancy-warning-signs and Ramadan ("لا تستطيع femio"). Pick one (masculine, as in "التطبيق") and apply it set-wide.
6. **Latin "PCOS" inside Arabic sentences** (pcos-signs x24, irregular-cycles x6, pcos-and-fertility, trying-to-conceive). After the first full term with the acronym in brackets, use "المتلازمة". The same applies to unexpanded organisation names at first mention: ACOG and NHS in fertile-window, pcos-and-fertility, trying-to-conceive, when-to-seek-fertility-help and when-to-take-pregnancy-test; FDA in when-to-take-pregnancy-test; ACOG in pregnancy-warning-signs; CDC and RCOG in the Ramadan guide. Expand each once per guide as the other guides already do.
7. **Calques are isolated, not systemic**: about one to three per guide (English word order, "تخلق" for "create", "ضيقة" for "narrow", "تعاني من" for "have"), and the Arabic is otherwise clear modern standard Arabic in the feminine, with no slang, no "يرجى" and no verdict language. Two ambiguities matter beyond style: "موصوفة" (reads as prescribed) and "إمساك الطعام" (constipation).
8. **Front matter is consistent with the lint, but the review state is the real gap.** All 11 Arabic files carry `written_by` and `medically_reviewed_by: PENDING`, `last_reviewed: null` and `reading_level_ok: false`. That is what `scripts/content-lint.mjs` (lines 253 to 277) requires. Do not copy "Dr. Khaled M." or 2026-08-04 from the English files. Ten Arabic guides are `status: published` while unreviewed; pregnancy-warning-signs and the Ramadan draft both point the reader to the "how we review" page. Titles, excerpts and `disclaimer_type` match the English meaning in all 11. In the Ramadan file, `internal_links` lacks `/ar/learn/pregnancy-warning-signs` (row 8.11).

## What I could not verify

- **Whether the English itself is medically right.** I compared Arabic to English only. I did not open a single source URL (ACOG, NHS, NICE, WHO, FDA, CDC, RCOG, the Ramadan papers). Every "matches" above means "matches the English file".
- **Reading level.** No Arabic readability formula exists in the repo. I counted sentence length by script (27 words or more flagged) and read for plain words. That is not a grade 6 to 8 measurement, so `reading_level_ok` must stay `false`.
- **Dialect fit.** Whether "الدورة" (for a period), "السونار", "تسمم الحمل" and "المرجع الديني" read right to Egyptian and Saudi women is my judgement, not tested with readers. "الدورة" is used for both "cycle" and "period" throughout the set, which the glossary separates (الدورة الشهرية vs الحيض); I left that out of the row counts and flag it here.
- **Rendering.** I did not render any page. Right-to-left mixing of Latin tokens (PCOS, hCG, NHS) and the proposed «» quotes are not visually checked.
- **Link targets.** I did not check that `/ar/learn/preconception-appointment`, `/ar/learn/endometriosis-and-fertility`, `/ar/glossary` and other routes exist.
- **Glucose units.** The Ramadan guide keeps 4 mmol/L from the NHS. Egyptian and Saudi labs usually report mg/dL (4 mmol/L is roughly 72 mg/dL by the standard factor of about 18). I did not source that conversion and did not put it in any row; the owner or clinician should decide whether to add it.
- **English anomalies I noticed but did not touch:** `when-to-take-pregnancy-test.md` has `date_modified` (2026-08-04) earlier than `date_published` (2026-08-05); `irregular-cycles-and-timing.md` is `status: published` with reviewer `PENDING`; the English pregnancy-test text says repeat "after several days" and later "after a week"; `pcos-signs.md` says dark skin patches "can be linked with insulin resistance", a claim that the 2026-10-28 PCOS review dropped from another article for lack of a source.
- **Lint.** I did not run `pnpm content-lint`. Any change to the doctor heading or the box label needs a lint run.
