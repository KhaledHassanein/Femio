# 04 · Clinical feature maturity audit (2026-11-26)

Reviewer role: clinician (obstetrics and gynaecology, reproductive endocrinology) with product-safety
experience. Read-only audit of worktree `judge-audit-bd6d27` at `fb45488`. Nothing was built or run:
no `flutter test`, no build. Every "VERIFIED" below means **verified by reading the code at the cited

## Overall feature maturity: 5 / 10

The cycle, pregnancy, after-birth and perimenopause views are real, rule-bound and well tested on
synthetic data, and the safety architecture (rules before any model, Help now in every header,
contraception refusal, no verdicts on records) is better than most consumer apps. Two things hold the
score down. First, **a woman cannot move between stages**. There is no "I'm pregnant" from trying to
conceive, no "my pregnancy ended", and no way to correct her due date, cycle length or "irregular"
answer after onboarding. The only exits are "I had my baby" and "Delete everything". That breaks the
wedge itself (trying to conceive, then pregnancy) and leaves pregnancy loss with no path at all.
Second, the **fertile window is drawn on the calendar without the mandated birth-control sentence
(MR-15)**, including for women who chose "Track my period".

**Clinical judgement:**
- Withholding everything after **one period with no usable length** is clinically legitimate. One date
  gives no cycle length, and a default of 28 days would be a guess presented as an estimate. That
  goes against rule 3.
- Withholding the **period range** for a stated length of **36-45 days** goes further than it needs
  to. MR-08 withholds only the *window* outside 21-35. MR-03 is written about the stated length's
  validity, but the website calculator already shows periods up to 45 (register MR-08 note). A wide
  low-confidence range for a 40-day stated cycle is honest and useful, especially for the PCOS wedge.
  Withholding the window is correct. Owner decision (CLIN-5).
- **Is she told why? Partly on Today, not on the calendar.**
  - Today: "Log your next period. When your period starts, log it and femio will predict the next
    one." (period_hero.dart:57; app_en.arb:70-71). It adds the irregular note only if she chose
    irregular. It never says "you chose Not sure" or "your length is outside 21-35", and it offers no
    way to enter one.
  - Calendar: when nothing can be predicted, the Next periods card and "Why this range?" are both
    hidden (calendar_screen.dart:375-379). The hint still reads "Estimates are shown in outline"
    (calendar_screen.dart:382; app_en.arb:220), which promises estimates that are not there.
- Hypothesis, not verified: if the owner's phone ran a build from before R8.10c (26 Nov), a stated
  length drew only one period and one window. If he answered "Not sure" or entered a length above 35,
  the empty calendar is the rule working, with no explanation. The lead's code-path diagnosis should
  confirm which.

## What is solid (for balance)

- Rule-first safety in Ask and Say it: crisis, urgent, ACOG heavy-bleeding sentence verbatim (MR-49),
  CDC postpartum signs for 365 days (MR-50), pregnancy signs, contraception refusal. All are word
  rules in four languages, before any model (safety.dart:195-239).
- Rule 3 in cycle modes: ranges with confidence, low confidence dashed, no past range drawn
  (day_marks.dart:255), "only the next period" when irregular, "no confident window" with the
  reason (prediction_sheets.dart:55-61).
- Rule 1 in the record: "never says whether a result is normal" (app_en.arb:1813), signs shown as
  logged, "femio does not say whether or when you ovulated" (app_en.arb:1587). The MR-16 strip
  sentence is present (app_en.arb:1586).
- Perimenopause bleeding after a year and after-birth lochia handling are clinically correct and
  conservative.
