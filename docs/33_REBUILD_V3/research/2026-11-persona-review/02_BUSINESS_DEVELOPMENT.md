## 5 femio Plus candidates (ranked)

Running costs: no server holds her data, so almost everything below costs the owner only build time. Play takes a service fee on subscriptions (rate: not verified here; check the Play Console fee page). Cloud boost is the only per-use cost.

Cloud boost arithmetic from Google's current paid-tier prices (S18): a call of about 3,000 input and 500 output tokens costs about $0.0005 on Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite ($0.10 and $0.40 per million tokens) and about $0.002 on Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite ($0.30 and $2.50) (my arithmetic). The free tier lets Google use prompts to improve products (S18), which ADR-078/080 already treat as a reason to use the paid tier. Note: per the same page, Gemini 2.5 Flash routes to 3.5 Flash-Lite on 2026-12-01; confirm which model the app calls.

Recommended bundle (a judgement, untested): one Plus tier containing 1 to 4 plus the lifted Cloud boost cap. Yearly SAR 49.99 / EGP 249.99, with a lifetime option SAR 99 / EGP 399 (hypotheses; calibrate against Play's own regional price templates, since I could not verify purchasing power or Play regional ladders). Everything safety-related (red flags, Help now, all 110 guides, the free Visit summary, Ask femio basic answers) stays free (rule 2). Sell convenience and depth, not access. A plain explanation of why: the free app must already be good enough to earn trust, because she has to trust a privacy promise before paying for it.

Bold idea, one line: let the first Plus feature be the clinician PDF pack, because it is the one thing a phone-only app can do that a cloud app cannot sell as "safe to carry to your doctor", and it creates the referral loop in section 6.

## 7 Rejected ideas and the rule each breaks

| Idea | Rule broken |
|---|---|
| Paywall red flags, Help now, or any guide | Rule 2 |
| Ads or "remove ads" as Plus (ADR-080 removed ads) | Rule 4 / ADR-080 |
| Attribution or analytics SDK (AppsFlyer, Adjust) to measure installs | Rule 4 |
| Cloud sync, accounts or cross-device backup hosted by femio | Rule 4 |
| Community, chat, or "ask other women" | Rule 5 |
| Sexual-wellness content packs | Rule 5 |
| Contraception or "safe days" mode (Natural Cycles' territory) | Rules 1 and 3 |

| Sharing aggregated user data with employers, insurers or sponsors | Rule 4 |
