06 — Launching femio, A to Z

For Khaled. Plain words, in order, with what to paste. Nothing here needs a developer: when a step says "a session", open Claude Code in the repo and paste the line in quotes. Every step says how long it takes and what "done" looks like. The texts you paste into Google Play are in 04_GROWTH_PACK.md §4 (ASO pack) and 03_LEGAL_PACK.md §4 (Play answers); this guide points at them rather than repeating them.

The shape of it. A → get the accounts and keys (one evening). B → the website goes live (one session). C → the app reaches your phone (one session, then you). D → the closed test with 12 testers (14 days, mostly waiting). E → production access and the soft launch. F → the first 100 women. Dates: release candidate 9 Nov, closed test 11–25 Nov, soft launch the week of 30 Nov 2026 (D5). Everything slips day for day with the release candidate.

A · Accounts and keys (one evening, about 1 hour)

A1 · Google Play Console account (20 min, $25 once)

  1. Go to play.google.com/console and sign in with the Google account that will own femio for good (not a work account you might lose).
  2. Choose "Yourself" (personal developer account). Pay the one-time $25.
  3. Identity verification: Google asks for a government ID and may send a letter or a code. Use your legal name and your Cairo address. This can take a few days; start it now.
  4. Done when: the Console shows "Verified" on the account.

Why personal and not an organisation account: you are the only person in every role; an organisation account needs a D-U-N-S number and company documents. The cost of a personal account is the closed-test rule in step D.

A2 · The signing key (10 min, once, keep it forever)

The key proves every future update comes from you. Lose it and femio has to start as a new app.

  1. Open PowerShell on your PC and run the keytool command in 05_RELEASE_RUNBOOK.md §2. It asks for a password and your name. Choose a long passphrase and save it in your password manager under "femio upload key".
  2. Copy the .jks file to a second place (an offline drive or the password manager's attachments).
  3. Copy apps/mobile/android/key.properties.example to apps/mobile/android/key.properties and type the path and passwords in. This file is ignored by git; never commit it.
  4. Done when: flutter build appbundle --release in apps/mobile finishes without "debug".

A3 · The three legal placeholders (5 min)

Open apps/web/src/lib/legal/placeholders.ts and fill in: the legal entity (KM Digital Labs and its registration number or your name as a sole trader), the privacy contact email (one you read), and the effective date (the launch week). A session can do this if you tell it the values.

A4 · Close the old cloud (10 min)

  1. Supabase: supabase.com → project → Settings → General → Pause project. If you ever edited translations in the old CMS portal, run the export line in docs/runbooks/OWNER_ACTIONS.md #4 first.
  2. Vercel: delete the femio-cms project. Keep femio (the website).
  3. In the femio Vercel project → Settings → Environment Variables: delete everything except NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL. (A session can list what is there if you paste a screenshot.) Done when: the Supabase dashboard says Paused and Vercel shows one project.

B · The website goes live (one session, 1 hour)

  1. Tell a session: "Merge the v3 branch into main and deploy". It runs the gate, merges, pushes; Vercel builds main. (The branch today: claude/femio-repo-study-planning-88d7c9.)
  2. On your phone, open femioapp.com/ar/calculators/fertile-window: tap a day; the window draws on the calendar; the Hijri date shows under each day.
  3. Open femioapp.com/ar/privacy: your entity, email and date appear where the highlighted placeholders were.
  4. Open femioapp.com/en/help-now: choose Egypt; the numbers appear with their sources.
  5. Share any article link in WhatsApp: the preview card shows the plum "femio" image.
  6. Google Search Console (optional, 10 min): search.google.com/search-console → add property femioapp.com → verify with the HTML tag → paste the token into Vercel as NEXT_PUBLIC_GOOGLE_SITE_VERIFICATION → submit https://femioapp.com/sitemap.xml. Done when: steps 2–5 look right on your phone.

C · The app reaches your phone (one session + 30 min of you)

  1. Tell a session: "Build the release candidate APK and bundle for R4.12". It bumps the version, builds both files, and tells you where the APK is (apps/mobile/build/app/outputs/flutter-apk/app-release.apk).
  2. Copy the APK to your phone (USB, or send it to yourself) and install it. Android will warn about an unknown source; allow it once.
  3. Run the six checks in 05_RELEASE_RUNBOOK.md §4 (onboarding in Arabic, PIN, backup, delete and restore, airplane mode, both themes). Write what is wrong in one message to a session: "On the Today screen in Arabic, …". Each round is one session.
  4. Done when: you would hand this phone to your wife or sister without explaining anything.

D · Play Console: listing, forms, closed test (2 hours of you, then 14 days)

D1 · Create the app (10 min)

Console → Create app → name femio → default language Arabic (Saudi Arabia), then add English, French and Spanish listings → app (not game) → free → accept the declarations.

D2 · Store listing (30 min)

Open docs/store/play-listing.ar.md and docs/store/play-listing.en.md beside the Console and paste:

  • Title (29 chars AR / 28 EN) and short description from the top of each file.
  • Full description AR and EN. The ship-gated lines are already resolved against the build (docs/store/README.md says which); fill the support email first. Keep the "not a medical device" paragraph; Google requires it. pnpm store-lint checks the limits.
  • Screenshots: 8 per language, from the real app (a session makes them with flutter test --update-goldens; captions in §4.4). The Arabic set first.
  • Feature graphic 1024×500 (§4.5) and the icon (apps/web/public/icons/icon-512.png).
  • Category Health & Fitness; contact email the one from A3; privacy policy URL https://femioapp.com/ar/privacy.

D3 · The forms (30 min, answer exactly as written)

Console → Policy → App content:

  • Privacy policy: the URL above.
  • Ads: No.
  • App access: all features available without special access.
  • Content rating: the IARC questionnaire; answer literally (03_LEGAL_PACK.md §4.4).
  • Target audience: 18 and over; tick "restrict minor access"; no, it does not appeal to children.
  • Data safety: the answers in 03_LEGAL_PACK.md §4.1, line by line. Collects: No. Shares: No. Deletion: Yes, in Settings.
  • Health apps declaration: Period tracking; Reproductive and sexual health; Medical reference and education. Nothing under medical device or clinical decision support (03_LEGAL_PACK.md §4.2).
  • Government apps / Financial features / News: No.

D4 · Find the 12 testers (your messages, 2 days)

Google requires 12 people who keep the app installed for 14 consecutive days before a personal account may publish. Aim for 16, because one who uninstalls on day 10 restarts the clock. Send the message in 04_GROWTH_PACK.md §5.5 (Egyptian Arabic, Gulf Arabic, English versions) to friends, family, colleagues and your clinic's staff. Ask each for the Gmail address they use on their phone.

D5 · Start the closed test (20 min)

Console → Testing → Closed testing → create track → upload the .aab a session built → release notes (two lines, AR and EN) → add the testers' emails (or a Google Group) → Start rollout. Send every tester the opt-in link the Console shows. Write the start date in docs/33_REBUILD_V3/PROGRESS.md R5.4.

D6 · During the 14 days

  • Every message from a tester goes to a session as it is ("Mona says the Hijri date is one day off"). The session fixes, gates, and tells you when a new bundle is ready; upload it to the same track. Updates do not restart the 14 days; uninstalls do.
  • Check the Console weekly: Testing → Closed testing → the tester count must stay ≥ 12.

E · Production access and the soft launch (about a week of waiting)

  1. On day 15, Console → Apply for production access. Describe the test honestly: who the testers were, what you fixed, what the app does. Google usually answers in about seven days.
  2. When approved: Production → Create release → upload the latest .aab → Countries: Egypt and Saudi Arabia first (add the rest later) → roll out.
  3. Tell a session: "The app is live at ; wire the store link". It sets PLAY_STORE_URL in apps/web/src/lib/site.ts, redeploys, and every "Get the app" button on the site goes to Play.
  4. Done when: a stranger in Riyadh can search "femio" on Play and install it.

F · The first 100 women (6 weeks)

The plan, channels, calendar and the one-question feedback link are in 04_GROWTH_PACK.md §5. In short: QR cards in the clinic, a WhatsApp Channel, Egyptian Facebook groups, three posts a week each tied to one article or calculator, and the Ramadan set live by 11 January 2027. You measure with Play Console installs and the feedback link, not with analytics in the app.

If something goes wrong

Symptom Do
Play rejects the bundle: "Health apps declaration" Reopen D3 and tick the three features; re-submit
Play rejects: "Data safety does not match" Tell a session: "Play says Data safety mismatch"; it re-runs the privacy tripwire and tells you what changed
A tester cannot install: "not available in your country" Add the country under Testing → Countries
You lost the keystore Stop. Tell a session; the app must be re-created under a new package name
A tester's records vanished They uninstalled; records live only on the phone. Restore from their backup file if they saved one
The website shows the old design Vercel built an older commit; tell a session "redeploy main"
