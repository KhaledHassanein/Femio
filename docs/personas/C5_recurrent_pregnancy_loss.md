## B. Needs & questions (voice-of-user)

Mined from r/recurrentmiscarriage, r/ttcafterloss, r/CautiousBB, Tommy's / RCOG patient forums, and app-store reviews. Phrasing is representative/composite (community norms; not quoting individuals).

**The core JTBD (RPL):**
1. *"Why does this keep happening to me?"* — a plain-language map of **possible** causes and the honest fact that **~50% stay unexplained**.
2. *"What tests should I have — and what do they actually check?"* (the RPL workup: bloods, clotting/antiphospholipid, thyroid, uterine imaging, karyotype/POC testing).
3. *"Is it my fault? Was it the coffee / the lifting / the stress?"* (guilt and self-blame — needs gentle, evidence-based reassurance without dismissal).
4. *"'Unexplained' — so am I just supposed to try again and hope?"* (the loneliness of no answer; what supportive care actually offers).
5. *"What do progesterone / aspirin / heparin actually do, and are they for me?"* (wants to **understand** her clinician's plan — not to self-prescribe).
6. *"How do I get through the next pregnancy without falling apart?"* (pregnancy-after-loss anxiety; getting to each milestone).
7. *"How do I prepare for my RPL appointment so I don't forget everything?"* (appointment prep: history, dates, questions, results in one place).
8. *"How do I track this cycle without it becoming an obsessive countdown to more heartbreak?"* (gentle, opt-out-able tracking).
9. *"My partner seems fine — are they? How do we grieve together?"* (partner grief asymmetry).
10. *"When is the 'right' time to try again?"* (physical + emotional readiness — an educational discussion, not a rule).

**Emotional arc — design must respect this (it is non-linear and recurs in waves):**
- **Acute loss:** shock, physical recovery, raw grief. **Any tracking/pregnancy surface is a wound.** This is the moment apps do maximum harm.
- **Searching for answers:** channelling grief into research and the workup; craving information and control; high engagement, high fragility.
- **Waiting / between:** anniversaries, would-be due dates, and "trying again?" ambivalence; grief returns in waves — *"don't assume I'm over it."*
- **Trying again (TTC-after-loss):** hope laced with dread; every symptom scrutinized; the two-week wait is amplified by trauma.
- **Pregnant again (PAL):** not joy but **"cautiously"** — white-knuckling from milestone to milestone (heartbeat, past-the-last-loss gestation, viability). See §J → D2 anxiety-aware mode.

**Anti-needs / trust-breakers (what causes deep harm, then churn):**
- **Insensitive automated reminders after a loss** — "time to take a pregnancy test," due-date countdowns, "your baby is the size of a…," or being **forced to log a loss as a 'period'** to silence the app. Community reports these as re-traumatizing (see §C).
- **Toxic positivity:** "just relax," "everything happens for a reason," "at least you can get pregnant," "it'll happen." Flattens grief; drives her out.
- **Doom-framing:** dwelling on worst-case statistics, framing her as broken, over-medicalizing the unexplained. The evidence (**~75% eventual live birth with supportive care** even after unexplained RPL) must be present *without* toxic positivity.
- **False certainty:** any prediction dressed as a promise ("this one will stick").
- **Data-privacy creep:** loss and pregnancy data are exceptionally sensitive (legal/insurance/social risk). Category history of leaks (see §K) makes mistrust rational.

**Feature requests recurring in reviews/forums:**
- A real **"loss" state** that instantly stops all pregnancy/fertility nudges and preserves data without forcing "period."
- A **grief-aware** home that acknowledges the loss and doesn't perform cheerfulness.
- A **workup tracker / results binder** and an **appointment-prep** builder.
- **Milestone-based** (not day-by-day) reassurance for the next pregnancy, with an anxiety-aware "check-in" instead of a fruit-size countdown.
- Control over **dates** (mute would-be due dates, loss anniversaries — or gently mark them, her choice).
- Partner grief support that doesn't assume the partner is fine.

## D. Clinical grounding (cited)

> All claims below are patient-education framing. Femio content is medically reviewed and cites primary sources. Femio does **not** diagnose, treat, or prevent; it educates and helps users organize information for their clinician.

**Definition (note the guideline divergence — surface it honestly):**
- **ASRM** defines RPL as **two or more failed clinical pregnancies**, and **excludes** confirmed **ectopic** and **molar** pregnancies. Evaluation may be considered after two, and is warranted. [ASRM RPL Committee Opinion]
- **ESHRE** defines RPL as the **loss of two or more pregnancies** and does not require them to be consecutive; it notes the definition may be extended/constricted per context. [ESHRE Guideline: RPL]
- Older **RCOG** framing referenced **three or more** consecutive losses; guidance is converging toward evaluation after two. Femio presents the range and always ends "your clinician decides when to investigate." [RCOG; ESHRE]

**Possible causes (educational map — most losses are sporadic; RPL warrants a look):**
- **Embryonic chromosomal abnormalities** are the **single most common** cause of miscarriage — roughly **~50%** of miscarriages involve the embryo having the wrong number of chromosomes (often sporadic, age-related). [NHS; Tommy's; StatPearls]
- **Inherited parental chromosomal rearrangement** (e.g., balanced translocation) accounts for roughly **3–5%** of RPL couples. [Tommy's; StatPearls]
- **Antiphospholipid syndrome (APS)** — the most important **treatable** cause; reported in a wide range (~**8–42%** across series/definitions) of RPL. ["sticky blood"; affects placental blood flow] [StatPearls; NHS; RCOG]
- **Uterine/anatomical anomalies** (e.g., septate uterus, adhesions) — present in roughly **~13%** of RPL in pooled data; a uterine-cavity evaluation is offered in unexplained RPL. [StatPearls; ASRM]
- **Endocrine / thyroid** (thyroid dysfunction, poorly controlled diabetes, and other hormonal factors) are associated with increased loss risk. [NHS; Tommy's; ASRM]
- **Other / contributory:** cervical insufficiency (2nd-trimester loss), certain infections/immune and inherited-thrombophilia factors (evidence mixed), and lifestyle factors. [StatPearls; RCOG]
- **~50% of RPL remains UNEXPLAINED after a full workup.** This is the central, must-communicate truth — framed with the hopeful counterweight below, never as doom. [ESHRE; ASRM; PMC]

**The standard evaluation ("workup" — education only, to prepare for the clinic):**
- **History & exam** (loss pattern/gestations, family/medical history). [ASRM; ESHRE]
- **Antiphospholipid antibody testing** (lupus anticoagulant, anticardiolipin, anti-β2-glycoprotein-I). [ASRM; ESHRE; RCOG]
- **Uterine cavity evaluation** (e.g., ultrasound/saline sonography/hysteroscopy/HSG as indicated). [ASRM; ESHRE]
- **Endocrine testing** (e.g., TSH/thyroid; others per clinical picture). [ASRM]
- **Genetic testing** — **parental karyotyping** on a **risk-assessed/individualized** basis (ESHRE/RCOG) rather than universally, and/or **testing products of conception (POC)** after a loss. [ESHRE; RCOG; ASRM]
- Many empirically offered add-ons are **not** evidence-supported for unexplained RPL — Femio must not imply a menu of "fixes." [ESHRE; ASRM]

**Supportive-care evidence (the hope, honestly stated):**
- Women with **unexplained** RPL have a **good prognosis**: roughly **~75%** go on to a successful pregnancy **with supportive care alone**. [Human Reproduction / ESHRE-cited reviews]
- Early-pregnancy **supportive care / "tender loving care" (TLC)** — reassurance, frequent contact, early scans, psychological support — is associated with better outcomes in observational studies (e.g., Stray-Pedersen **86% vs 33%**; Clifford et al. **26% vs 51%** miscarriage), though the evidence is **non-randomized** and should be framed as *supportive, not proven-causal*. [Human Reproduction; British Journal of Midwifery; PubMed 1799343]
- **Empirical adjuvant drug treatment** for **unexplained** RPL generally **does not improve** outcomes over supportive care alone — guard against implying medication is a fix. [Human Reproduction; ESHRE]
- **Medications tied to specific diagnoses (education only):** RCOG describes clinician-managed low-dose aspirin plus heparin for recurrent miscarriage associated with **APS**. Progesterone is not a universal recurrent-miscarriage treatment; NICE limits its recommendation to scan-confirmed intrauterine pregnancy with early bleeding and a previous miscarriage. Thyroid treatment follows the user's diagnosed condition and prescriber. Femio explains why a clinician may use a category and never advises starting, stopping, or dosing. ([RCOG](https://www.rcog.org.uk/for-the-public/browse-our-patient-information/recurrent-miscarriage/); [NICE NG126, updated June 2026](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng126/chapter/management-of-miscarriage); [reviewed rule](../compliance/REPRODUCTIVE_TREATMENT_SAFETY.md); reviewed Mohamed K., 23 July 2026.)

**Psychosocial grounding:**
- RPL carries high rates of **anxiety, depression, and grief**; guidelines recommend **validating the experience, screening for mood disorders, and offering referral** to mental-health support for the woman **and** partner. [ASRM; ESHRE]

*Femio applies all of the above as **educational preparation and organization**, always ending "discuss with your clinician" — never as a diagnosis or treatment plan.*

Flagged for medical-review sign-off before ship: exact definitional thresholds (2 vs 3), each cause percentage, the supportive-care/TLC figures, and every medication statement — cite the live primary text per locale at publish. **[NEEDS SOURCE VERIFICATION AT PUBLISH]** for any figure stated without a per-locale reviewed citation.

## E. Feature set (prioritized)

**P0 — Must-have (defines the persona; ship first):**
1. **Loss-aware core state ("Gentle Mode")** — a first-class **loss** state that, the instant a loss is reported, **suppresses every pregnancy/fertility/countdown nudge**, removes cheerful surfaces, preserves all data (no "log it as a period" hack), and shows a quiet, acknowledging home. *This is the single most important feature in the entire spec.* (See §G reminder-suppression contract.)
2. **RPL cause explainer (referenced, non-doom)** — plain-language map of possible causes + the honest **~50% unexplained** truth, paired with the **~75% eventual success** counterweight. No self-blame framing.
3. **Workup / results binder** — organize her tests and results (APS/clotting, thyroid, uterine imaging, karyotype/POC) in one place; understand what each checks; store result documents (encrypted, user-deletable). Educational, non-diagnostic.
4. **Appointment-prep builder** — auto-compiles loss history (dates/gestations), current meds/supplements, symptoms, and a **question list** into a shareable/exportable summary for her RPL consult.
5. **Gentle cycle / TTC-after-loss tracking** — opt-in, low-pressure tracking with an explicit "keep it minimal" setting; anxiety-aware TWW; no obsessive countdowns; easy pause.
6. **Symptom & medication log (education-only framing)** — log progesterone, low-dose aspirin, LMWH/heparin, levothyroxine, supplements, symptoms; **adherence reminders for meds she is already prescribed**, with clear "not medical advice — follow your clinician's plan" wording. Never suggests initiating/stopping/dosing.
7. **Grief-aware emotional support + routing** — validating (not fixing) content, mood check-ins/screening with clinician-referral framing, and **routing to specialized loss support** (Tommy's, Miscarriage Association, PSI, PAL communities, local resources). Femio is **not** therapy.

**P1 — Strong differentiators (fast follow):**
8. **Pregnancy-After-Loss (PAL) anxiety-aware mode** — if she becomes pregnant again, a **milestone-paced** (not fruit-size-countdown) experience: heartbeat, past-last-loss gestation, viability; "reassurance check-ins," honest uncertainty, and a "how are you *really*" surface. (§J → D2.)
9. **Date-control center** — she chooses whether would-be due dates and loss anniversaries are **muted, gently marked, or hidden**; nothing auto-fires on these dates without her setting.
10. **Partner mode (grief-asymmetry-aware)** — optional partner view that supports *the partner's* grief too (not "here's how to help her"), consent-gated and revocable; recognizes solo users equally.
11. **"Is it my fault?" myth-vs-evidence module** — targeted, gentle content countering common self-blame (coffee, lifting, stress, sex) with cited evidence.
12. **Data export (PDF)** — hand her full history to a new clinician or specialist; portability = trust.

**P2 — Nice-to-have / later:**
13. Moderated, privacy-safe peer support (loss/PAL-specific; carefully designed to avoid announcement-triggers).
14. Localized grief content and clinic directories per market (AR modesty- and stigma-aware).
15. Anniversary/remembrance rituals (opt-in, culturally sensitive).
16. Custom trackables for her specific protocol.

**Explicitly OUT of scope for C5 (guardrails):**
- **Any instruction to start/stop/change medication or dose** (progesterone, aspirin, LMWH, levothyroxine). **Never.** Education + "discuss with your clinician" only.
- **Diagnosing** the cause of her losses, or telling her whether a future pregnancy will succeed. Screeners *flag & route*, never diagnose (§K).
- Replacing mental-health care — Femio supports and **routes**, it does not treat.
- Any contraceptive / "safe day" framing. **Never.**

## G. Tools, logs & alerts (compliant framing; grief-sensitive; instant reminder suppression on loss)

**Logs (inputs):**
- Loss records {date, gestation, type, notes} (molar/ectopic tagged & excluded from RPL logic), workup results {test, value, date, document_ref}, current-cycle tracking (opt-in/minimal), symptoms, **meds/supplements** {name, dose *as prescribed by clinician*, schedule}, mood/well-being check-ins, appointment notes & questions, betas/scan dates (PAL), key dates (due-date, anniversaries) with per-date display preference.

**Tools:**
- Gentle-Mode controller (the loss state), workup/results binder, appointment-prep compiler, question-list builder, medication-adherence reminders (prescribed-only), anxiety-aware TWW helper, PAL milestone tracker, date-control center, mood screener (non-diagnostic), grief-resource router, PDF export.

**THE REMINDER-SUPPRESSION CONTRACT (non-negotiable, safety-critical):**
- On a reported loss (or user tapping "I've had a loss"), the system **immediately and atomically**:
  1. **Cancels/suppresses ALL** pregnancy, fertility-window, TWW, "time to test," countdown, fruit-size, and celebratory notifications — **before the next render**, with no lag window.
  2. **Does NOT require** the user to reclassify the loss as a "period" or reset her data to stop prompts.
  3. Switches home to a quiet, acknowledging surface; hides cheerful widgets.
  4. Applies her **date preferences** (mute/mark/hide due-date & anniversaries).
  5. Preserves all data intact and reversible; nothing is silently deleted.
- **Fail-safe/default:** if state is ambiguous, **default to silence** — never risk a wrongful cheerful reminder. Suppression is tested as a P0 safety path (see §K). Any med-adherence reminder for a *prescribed* medication continues only if clinically ongoing and the user keeps it on.

**Alerts / nudges (all opt-in, tone-calibrated, revocable; Gentle-Mode-aware):**
- Med adherence (prescribed only): "Reminder for the medication you and your clinician planned — follow their instructions."
- Appointment prep: "Your consult is in 3 days — want to review your questions and results?"
- Gentle check-in (Gentle Mode): "Thinking of you today. Want to note how you're doing?" (never a demand; easily muted).
- PAL milestone (anxiety-aware, opt-in): "You've reached [milestone]. However you're feeling is okay." — no fruit-size, no false promise.
- Date-aware (only if she opted to *mark* a date): a soft, private acknowledgment — never automatic, never public.

**Mandatory compliance wording on relevant surfaces:**
- *"Femio is for information and tracking. We don't diagnose, and we never tell you to start, stop, or change any medication — follow your clinician's plan."*
- Screener outputs: *"This is a screening tool, not a diagnosis. Please discuss results with a clinician."*
- Predictive surfaces (if any): *"An estimate based on your data — not a guarantee."*
- No "safe/infertile day" language — **ever**.

## I. Personalization rules (wizard → home config; grief-sensitive; hidden-but-reachable)

**Onboarding wizard (branch into C5) — trauma-informed, minimal, skippable:**
1. "What brings you to Femio?" → *I've experienced pregnancy loss.* (Offered plainly, without euphemism or forced detail.)
2. Gentle, **optional** capture of loss history (count/dates/gestations) — *"share only what you want; you can add later."* Sets `pregnancy_losses[]`, `rpl_loss_count`, and **enters Gentle Mode by default**.

4. "Are you working with a clinician / in a workup?" → enables results binder + appointment prep.
5. "Are you taking any medications for this?" → med log (prescribed-only framing).
6. Date preferences → would-be due dates / anniversaries: mute / mark / hide.
7. "Involve a partner?" (grief-asymmetry-aware) or *I'm doing this on my own* → equal-weight solo path.
8. Consent & privacy choices (heightened sensitivity notice).

**Home config for C5 (varies by state):**
- **Gentle Mode (acute/grieving):** quiet acknowledging home; grief content + resources; **zero** pregnancy/fertility widgets; only opt-in gentle check-in.
- **Seeking answers:** hero = **workup binder + appointment prep**; cause explainer; supportive-care hope content.
- **TTC-after-loss:** minimal, low-pressure tracking + anxiety-aware TWW; hope-and-fear-aware tone.
- **PAL (pregnant again):** milestone tracker + "how are you really" check-in; **no** fruit-size countdown; honest uncertainty.
- **Hidden-but-reachable:** full lifespan library one tap away — but nothing cheerful is *pushed* while Gentle Mode/PAL-anxiety is active.

**Adaptive tone (the tightrope):** always **validate-and-inform** — hold grief and hope together. Never "just relax," "everything happens for a reason," or worst-case doom. When surfacing statistics, always pair the hard truth (~50% unexplained) with the honest hope (~75% eventual success), sourced.

## K. Compliance flags

1. **Reminder-suppression is a safety requirement, not a feature nicety (highest-priority guardrail).** A wrongful cheerful/pregnancy/countdown notification to a bereaved user is a serious harm. The §G suppression contract must be **atomic, pre-render, fail-safe-to-silence**, and covered by automated P0 safety tests and manual QA before any release touching notifications. **[NEEDS SOURCE — align to Femio notification-safety runbook at implementation.]**
2. **No diagnosis / treatment / prevention.** Femio never diagnoses the cause of losses, never tells a user whether a pregnancy will succeed, and **never** instructs starting/stopping/changing/dosing any medication (progesterone, aspirin, LMWH, levothyroxine). Med reminders are for **already-prescribed** regimens with "follow your clinician." Screeners flag & route only.
3. **Tone safety — no toxic positivity, no doom.** Content policy bans "just relax," "everything happens for a reason," "at least…," and worst-case doom-framing. Hard statistics are always paired with honest, sourced hope. Reviewed for emotional safety, not just accuracy.
4. **Uncertainty honesty.** ~50%-unexplained and non-randomized TLC/supportive-care evidence must be stated as such; no false promise that "this one will stick."
5. **Privacy-by-design / exceptional data sensitivity.** Loss, pregnancy, workup, and medication data carry legal/insurance/social risk. Learn from category history: **Flo (FTC 2021, data to Facebook/Google)** and **Premom (FTC 2023, data to analytics)**. No sharing with ad networks/third-party analytics; data minimization; explicit, granular, revocable consent; strict retention; user-deletable result documents; export on demand. **[NEEDS SOURCE — FTC primary docs; verify at implementation.]**
6. **GDPR / PDPL — special-category health data.** Lawful basis + explicit consent; DSAR/export/delete; regional data-residency (esp. AR/MENA where loss can also carry social/stigma risk). **[NEEDS SOURCE — align to Femio privacy skill / DPA.]**
7. **Partner mode consent + solo parity.** Partner sees only granted scope; consent logged, revocable; grief-asymmetry-aware; solo users are first-class, never nudged toward a partner.
8. **Not therapy — route, don't treat.** Mood screeners are non-diagnostic; Femio surfaces and links to qualified loss/mental-health support (Miscarriage Association, PSI, PAL, Tommy's, local services), and does not present itself as counseling.
9. **Medical review + citations.** All clinical content medically reviewed, cited to Tier-1 (NIH/NHS/MedlinePlus) + Tier-2 (ASRM/ESHRE/RCOG/ACOG) primary sources, grade 6–8, localized in 4 languages with RTL parity. Definitional thresholds, cause percentages, TLC/supportive-care figures, and every medication statement re-verified against live primary text at publish. **[NEEDS SOURCE VERIFICATION AT PUBLISH]**
10. **App-store health-app policy.** Meet Apple/Google health-app & medical-disclaimer requirements; no over-claiming; sensitive-content handling. **[NEEDS SOURCE — map to current store policies at submission.]**
