# Persona D2 — Pregnancy After Loss (PAL): Guarded Joy, Milestone-by-Milestone

> Femio persona spec. **Layered on D1 (uncomplicated pregnancy)** — D2 inherits the entire D1 pregnancy spine (week-by-week content, appointments, weight guidance, kick counter, contraction timer, red-flag safety layer) and re-skins it with an **anxiety-aware, loss-aware tone**. It is the **destination of the C5 (RPL) positive-test transition** and the general arrival point for *any* pregnancy following a prior miscarriage, stillbirth, neonatal, ectopic, molar, or termination-for-medical-reasons loss.
> Scope guardrail (inherited, non-negotiable): **wellness / education / tracking only. Never diagnose, treat, prevent, or contracept.** Red-flag symptoms get prominent "seek care now" framing, never a diagnosis. Screeners = *"screening — discuss with your clinician."*
> Tone guardrail: **hold hope and fear together. No toxic positivity, no doom. "However you feel is okay."**
> Persona code: **D2** · Cohort: Pregnancy (D) · Layered on: **D1** · Emotional lineage: **C5 → D2** · Last research pass: 2026-07-17

**Persona thesis.** D2 is where the D1 pregnancy engine meets the C5 loss-awareness engine. It is **not a lighter pregnancy** — clinically it may be entirely low-risk — it is an **emotionally higher-acuity** pregnancy. The single most important design truth: *reaching a milestone does not end the anxiety; it often relocates it to the next one.* Every other pregnancy app fails her by assuming the positive test is an ending (joy!) rather than the start of a marathon of dread. Femio wins D2 by pacing the entire pregnancy in **milestones, not countdowns**, by making "however you feel is okay" a structural default rather than a slogan, and by keeping the C5 loss-suppression safety machinery armed the whole way through in case of another loss.

## B. Needs & questions (voice-of-user)

Mined from r/pregnancyafterloss, r/ttcafterloss, r/CautiousBB, Tommy's rainbow-pregnancy stories, Pregnancy After Loss Support (PALS), the Miscarriage Association's "pregnancy after miscarriage" pages, and app-store reviews. Phrasing is representative/composite (community norms; not quoting individuals).

**The core JTBD (PAL):**
1. *"How do I get through this without falling apart between scans?"* — anxiety management, milestone by milestone.
2. *"Just get me to the next milestone."* — heartbeat, past my last loss's gestation, 12 weeks, viability (~24 wk), anatomy scan, term. Break the pregnancy into survivable steps.
3. *"Every time I go to the bathroom I brace for blood. Is [symptom] normal or is it happening again?"* — hypervigilant symptom-checking (→ honest "is this normal" + red-flag routing, never "your baby is fine").
4. *"I passed the week I lost my last baby — why am I MORE anxious now, not less?"* — the milestone-relief myth; new fears replace old ones.
5. *"Can I ask for an early reassurance scan? Will they take me seriously?"* — additional monitoring literacy (early pregnancy units, rainbow/specialist clinics).
6. *"I don't want to announce, buy anything, or get attached — is that normal?"* — guarded attachment, delayed announcing, not wanting a "countdown."
7. *"I'm pregnant again but I'm still grieving my baby. Does that make me ungrateful?"* — co-existing grief + hope; guilt about both.
8. *"Please don't call it a 'rainbow baby' — it feels like it erases the one I lost."* — language sensitivity (some embrace "rainbow," some reject it — **let her choose**).
9. *"How do I count kicks / when do I worry about movement?"* — heightened fetal-movement vigilance, especially after stillbirth (→ inherits D1 kick-count, tuned).
10. *"My partner acts fine — are they? We're both terrified."* — partner anxiety asymmetry.

**Emotional arc — design must respect this (non-linear, milestone-anchored, recurs in waves):**
- **Positive test / very early:** not joy but **"here we go again"** — dread the moment the second line appears; the C5→D2 handoff moment (§J). Bracing, not celebrating.
- **To the heartbeat scan:** terror of a "silent" scan; the first big milestone.
- **Approaching the last-loss gestation:** the pivotal milestone. Anxiety peaks *around and just past* it — relief is real but brief, then re-targets.
- **Mid-pregnancy (anatomy scan, viability):** cautious loosening for some; for others (esp. after stillbirth) anxiety *deepens* toward the third trimester.
- **Third trimester (esp. post-stillbirth):** movement hypervigilance; countdown-to-birth dread; "will I actually bring a baby home?" — often the hardest stretch.
- **Throughout:** grief for the prior baby re-surfaces in waves; anniversaries and would-be due dates still land (inherited C5 date-control).

**Anti-needs / trust-breakers (deep harm → churn):**
- **Celebratory countdowns and fruit-size cards pushed at her** ("Congratulations! Only 200 days to go!" / "your baby is the size of a…") when she is white-knuckling day to day.
- **False reassurance** — "your baby is fine," "everything will be okay this time," "don't worry, it won't happen again." Never promise an outcome.
- **Toxic positivity** — "relax or you'll stress the baby," "just enjoy it," "at least you know you can get pregnant."
- **Milestone triumphalism** — treating a passed milestone as "you're safe now" (she knows she isn't).
- **Erasing the prior baby** — forced "rainbow" framing; treating this pregnancy as a fresh start that overwrites her loss.
- **Any lag or failure in loss-suppression** if this pregnancy also ends in loss — the C5 §G contract must stay armed the entire pregnancy.
- **Privacy creep** — loss + pregnancy data stacked together is exceptionally sensitive.

**Feature requests recurring in reviews/forums:**
- **Milestone-based pacing** (heartbeat / past-last-loss / viability / anatomy / term) instead of a day counter — *and the ability to set her own personal milestones, especially her last-loss gestation.*
- An **anxiety-aware "how are you really?"** check-in, not a fruit card.
- **Optional/hideable** development content and imagery (some want it, some can't look yet — **her toggle**).
- **"However you feel is okay"** framing everywhere; permission to not be excited.
- **Reassurance-scan literacy** — how to ask, what early pregnancy units / specialist clinics offer.
- **Language control** — opt in or out of "rainbow baby"; name/acknowledge the prior baby if she wants.
- Loss-suppression that **stays armed** — instant, reliable quiet if this pregnancy also ends.

## D. Clinical grounding (cited)

> All claims below are patient-education framing. Femio content is medically reviewed and cites primary sources. Femio does **not** diagnose, treat, prevent, or contracept; it educates and helps her organize information for her clinician. **D2 inherits all of D1's clinical grounding** (prenatal schedule, IOM weight-gain bands, CDC urgent warning signs, kick-counting method, nutrition/supplements, screening-vs-diagnostic) — only the **PAL-specific additions** are below.

**Prior loss meaningfully raises perinatal anxiety/depression risk (the core PAL clinical truth).**
- Women who have experienced a previous perinatal loss report **significantly higher anxiety and depression symptoms in a subsequent pregnancy** than those without prior loss. [PMC case-cohort (Scandinavia); PubMed 21372060 — previous prenatal loss predicts perinatal depression/anxiety]
- Distress after stillbirth/neonatal death carries elevated **post-traumatic stress and emotional suppression** into the next pregnancy. [PMC longitudinal survey; PMC multicentre cohort]
- → App framing: PAL anxiety is **expected and valid, not a disorder to be ashamed of**; Femio normalizes it, screens (non-diagnostically), and routes to support. Never pathologize; never dismiss.

**Mental-health screening in pregnancy (screening, not diagnosis).**
- ACOG recommends screening **for both depression and anxiety** using standardized validated tools (e.g., EPDS, PHQ-9/GAD-7) at least once in the perinatal period — at the initial prenatal visit, later in pregnancy, and postpartum. A prior loss is a recognized risk factor warranting attention. [ACOG Clinical Practice Guideline (2023); ACOG Committee Opinion #757]
- → Femio surfaces mood/anxiety check-ins framed strictly as **"this is a screening — discuss results with your clinician,"** with EPDS-Q10 (self-harm) any-non-zero → **immediate crisis-resource path** regardless of total (inherited universal handler). Never a diagnosis.

**Additional monitoring & reassurance scans (education, availability varies by system/region).**
- After a prior miscarriage, a woman can ask her GP or **Early Pregnancy Unit (EPAU/EPAC)** about an **early reassurance scan** in the next pregnancy — typically offered around **6–8 weeks**, and often prioritized after **recurrent (≥3) loss**; availability depends on local policy and clinical reason. [Tommy's; Miscarriage Association; RCOG; NHS EPAU pages]
- **Specialist antenatal clinics for pregnancy after loss** (e.g., Tommy's **Rainbow Clinic** model, for those with prior stillbirth/neonatal death) provide extra scans, continuity, and psychological support. Observational evidence is encouraging: in one study, **~1 in 50** Rainbow-Clinic pregnancies ended in stillbirth/second-trimester loss vs **~4 in 50** not attending, with lower very-preterm birth — framed as **supportive/observational, not a guarantee**. [Tommy's Rainbow Clinic; PMC — impact of a dedicated antenatal service after stillbirth]
- → Femio framing: *"Extra scans and specialist 'pregnancy after loss' or rainbow clinics may be available — ask your midwife or Early Pregnancy Unit. What's offered depends on your history and where you live."* Education + "discuss with your clinician"; never a promise of access or outcome.

**Fetal-movement awareness (heightened relevance after stillbirth — but honest about the evidence).**
- Getting to know the baby's movement pattern and reporting **any change/reduction the same day** is standard advice (inherited from D1). After stillbirth this vigilance is understandably intense.
- **Honesty caveat:** large trials/reviews (e.g., **AFFIRM**; Cochrane-type meta-analysis) found the effect of *encouraging awareness of reduced fetal movement* on stillbirth is **uncertain** — some signal for fewer NICU admissions/low-Apgar, but not a proven stillbirth reduction. Femio presents movement awareness as **"contact your provider the same day if movements change,"** never as a guarantee, and never implies the kick counter is a test of the baby's wellbeing. [PubMed 36481411; Tommy's AFFIRM]

**Supportive-care / "TLC" hope, honestly stated (carried from C5).**
- Even after unexplained recurrent loss, prognosis for a future successful pregnancy is **good (~75% with supportive care)**; early-pregnancy supportive care (reassurance, frequent contact, early scans, psychological support) is associated with better outcomes in **observational** (non-randomized) studies. Pair every hard statistic with honest hope; never a false promise "this one will stick." [Human Reproduction; British Journal of Midwifery — TLC clinic; ESHRE/ASRM]

**Medications she may already be on (education only — inherited C5 guardrail).**
- She may continue a clinician-prescribed regimen into this pregnancy: **progesterone** (some benefit signal for those with prior loss + early bleeding — nuanced, not universal), **low-dose aspirin ± LMWH/heparin** for **APS**, **levothyroxine** for thyroid dysfunction. Femio provides **adherence reminders for already-prescribed** medicines and explains what they are, and **never** tells her to start, stop, change, or dose anything. **[NEEDS SOURCE — cite live primary text per drug at publish (e.g., PRISM trial for progesterone; APS anticoagulation guidance).]**

*Flagged for medical-review sign-off before ship:* the PAL anxiety/depression risk statements, the reassurance-scan timing/eligibility (varies sharply by health system — **localize** for MENA/AR, UK, EU, US), the Rainbow-Clinic outcome figures (observational), the AFFIRM/movement-awareness nuance, and every medication statement. **[NEEDS SOURCE VERIFICATION AT PUBLISH]** for any figure stated without a per-locale reviewed citation.

12. **Partner mode (anxiety-asymmetry-aware).** Optional partner view that supports *the partner's* anxiety/grief too; consent-gated, revocable; solo users first-class.
13. **Grief-aware emotional support + routing.** Validating (not fixing) content for grief that re-surfaces during pregnancy; routing to PALS, Tommy's, Miscarriage Association, PSI, local services. Femio is **not** therapy.

**P2 — Nice-to-have / later:**

**Explicitly OUT of scope for D2 (guardrails):**

## F. Content modules

> Authored per femio-medical-content skill (grade 6–8, referenced, 4 locales, RTL-first for AR). **Inherits all D1 content modules** (this-week development [now toggle-gated], trimester primers, symptom explainers, appointments & tests, nutrition, weight guidance, movement & kick counting, warning-signs hub, preparing for birth). **D2-specific additions:**
- **"Pregnancy after loss — you're not alone in feeling this way"** — normalizes guarded joy, dread, and grief that co-exists with hope; validates *not* feeling excited; the anxiety is expected, not a failing.
- **"One milestone at a time"** — reframes the pregnancy as survivable steps (heartbeat, past-last-loss, viability, anatomy, term); **honestly names that passing a milestone often moves the anxiety rather than ending it** — so she isn't blindsided by feeling worse after "good news."
- **"Is this normal, or is it happening again?"** — gentle, cited guidance on common early symptoms (spotting, cramping, symptom fluctuation) *paired with clear red-flag routing* — never "your baby is fine," always "here's what's commonly benign, and here's when to seek care today."
- **"Asking for extra reassurance"** — how to talk to your GP/midwife/EPAU about an early scan or specialist/rainbow clinic; what they may offer; that access varies by history and region.
- **"When grief shows up during pregnancy"** — grief for the baby you lost can return in waves now; that's normal and doesn't mean you love this pregnancy less.
- **"On the word 'rainbow'"** — why some parents embrace "rainbow baby" and others reject it; *your language is your choice* (drives the §E5 setting).
- **"For partners"** — partner anxiety after loss is real and often hidden; you can both be terrified and supporting each other.
- **"Movement and your peace of mind"** — the kick-counter, honestly: a way to know *your* baby's pattern and a prompt to call the same day if it changes — **not a test that the baby is okay** (AFFIRM-honest).
- **"When to reach out for more support"** — signs anxiety/low mood needs more help; warm links to PAL/mental-health resources; crisis resources always reachable.
- **Reachable-but-hidden library:** the full D1 pregnancy library + full lifespan content stays searchable — she is supported, not boxed in — but **nothing celebratory is ever pushed** while D2 anxiety-aware mode is active.

## I. Personalization rules (wizard → home config; anxiety-aware; hidden-but-reachable)

**Arrival into D2 (two paths — trauma-informed, minimal):**
- **From C5 (or any loss history) on a positive test** — the primary path (§J). The C5 handoff **does not celebrate loudly**; it offers the switch to D2 anxiety-aware mode, carries loss history forward, and continues prescribed-med reminders.
- **From the wizard directly** — "I'm pregnant" branch + "any prior pregnancy loss?" → yes routes into D2 tone (per Master §4, Q4 "prior loss → D2 tone"). Gentle, optional capture of loss history and last-loss gestation; *"share only what you want; you can add later."*

**Onboarding capture (optional, skippable):**
1. Confirm pregnancy + EDD/LMP (or carry from C5/cycle history; "I'll add later" → generic anxiety-aware pregnancy home until set).
2. **Last-loss gestation** (optional) → seeds the pivotal personal milestone. *"If you'd like, tell us how far along you were before — we'll help you take this one milestone at a time."*
3. Content/imagery preferences → show or hide development content, fetal imagery, size comparisons, countdown (all **hideable; countdown/celebration off by default**).
4. Language preference → "rainbow baby" opt in/out; name/acknowledge prior baby (optional).
5. Any medications for this pregnancy? → prescribed-only med log.
6. Date preferences (would-be due dates / anniversaries: mute / mark / hide).
7. Partner? (anxiety-asymmetry-aware) or *I'm doing this on my own* → equal-weight solo path.
8. Consent & privacy (heightened-sensitivity notice).

**Home configuration (gestational-age-driven, anxiety-aware):**
- **Hero = next milestone**, leading with "however you feel is okay" — *not* a fruit card or day counter.
- **Early (positive test → heartbeat → last-loss gestation):** milestone tracker + "how are you really" check-in + reassurance-scan literacy + red-flag access; development content only if she opted in.
- **Mid (viability, anatomy scan):** milestone tracker; gentle development content per her toggle; honest "milestones move the anxiety" content surfaced.
- **T3 (esp. post-stillbirth):** movement awareness (anxiety-aware kick counter), birth-prep, warning-signs prominent; "will I bring my baby home" support content.
- **Loss-suppression armed at all times**; if triggered → instant Gentle Mode (→ E6).
- **Hidden-but-reachable:** full D1 pregnancy library + lifespan content one tap away — but nothing celebratory is *pushed* while anxiety-aware mode is active.

**Adaptive tone (the tightrope, inherited C5):** always **validate-and-inform** — hold grief and hope together. Never "just relax," "everything happens for a reason," "you're safe now," or worst-case doom. Pair every hard truth with honest, sourced hope; never promise an outcome.

**Blending with clinical modifiers (Master §8).** D2 is an **emotional layer that stacks on any D-track clinical persona.** If she also carries a condition (thyroid → D7, GDM → D3, HTN → D5/D6, multiples → D9, AMA → D13, etc.): most-acute-clinical emphasis wins the hero's *clinical strip*, but **tone stays anxiety-aware (D2)** and the loss-suppression net stays armed. Worked blend from Master: *pregnancy ⊕ thyroid ⊕ PAL* → hero = pregnancy milestones; strips = thyroid monitoring + PAL support; tone = anxiety-aware.

## K. Compliance flags

1. **Loss-suppression stays armed the entire pregnancy (highest-priority guardrail, inherited C5).** A wrongful celebratory/milestone/countdown notification to a PAL user who has just experienced another loss is a serious, unforgivable harm. The §G suppression contract must be **atomic, pre-render, fail-safe-to-silence**, covered by automated P0 safety tests + manual QA before any release touching notifications. **[NEEDS SOURCE — align to Femio notification-safety runbook at implementation.]**
2. **No diagnosis / treatment / prevention / contraception.** Femio never diagnoses, never interprets a scan/kick-count/mood-screen as a verdict, never promises an outcome ("your baby is fine," "you're safe now," "it won't happen again"), and **never** instructs starting/stopping/changing/dosing medication. Med reminders are for **already-prescribed** regimens. Screeners flag & route only.
3. **Tone safety — no toxic positivity, no doom, no forced joy.** Content policy bans "just relax," "everything happens for a reason," "at least…," "you're safe now past X," and worst-case doom-framing. Celebration and countdowns are **opt-in only**. "However you feel is okay" is a structural default. Reviewed for emotional safety, not just accuracy.
4. **Uncertainty honesty.** PAL anxiety-risk statements, reassurance-scan availability, Rainbow-Clinic (observational) figures, and AFFIRM/movement-awareness nuance stated as such; every hard statistic paired with honest, sourced hope; no false promise.
5. **Language autonomy.** "Rainbow baby" is **opt-in** (default off); she may name/acknowledge the prior baby; the new pregnancy **never erases** the loss.
6. **Privacy-by-design / exceptional data sensitivity.** Loss + pregnancy + medication data stacked carry legal/insurance/social risk (esp. AR/MENA stigma). No sharing with ad networks/third-party analytics; data minimization; explicit granular revocable consent; strict retention; user-deletable documents; export on demand. Category history (Flo FTC 2021; Premom FTC 2023) is the cautionary baseline. **[NEEDS SOURCE — FTC primary docs; verify at implementation.]**
7. **GDPR / PDPL — special-category health data.** Lawful basis + explicit consent; DSAR/export/delete; regional data-residency. **[NEEDS SOURCE — align to Femio privacy skill / DPA.]**
8. **Partner mode consent + solo parity.** Partner sees only granted scope; consent logged, revocable; anxiety-asymmetry-aware; solo users first-class.
9. **Not therapy — route, don't treat.** Anxiety/mood screeners non-diagnostic; Femio surfaces and links to qualified PAL/mental-health support (PALS, PSI, Miscarriage Association, Tommy's, local), does not present as counseling; crisis resources on any high mood-screen; EPDS-Q10 non-zero → immediate crisis path.
10. **Medical review + citations.** All clinical content medically reviewed, cited Tier-1 (NIH/NHS/MedlinePlus) + Tier-2 (ACOG/RCOG/Tommy's/ESHRE/ASRM), grade 6–8, localized in 4 languages with RTL parity. PAL anxiety-risk figures, reassurance-scan eligibility/timing (localize per health system), Rainbow-Clinic figures, AFFIRM nuance, and every medication statement re-verified against live primary text at publish. **[NEEDS SOURCE VERIFICATION AT PUBLISH]**
