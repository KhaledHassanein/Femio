## B. Needs & questions (voice-of-user)

Mined from r/TryingForABaby, r/ttcafterloss, r/CautiousBB, r/Anxiety, and app-store/press reviews of fertility apps. Phrasing is representative/composite (community norms; not quoting individuals).

**The core JTBD (anxious TTC):**
1. *"How do I survive the two-week wait without losing my mind?"* — the TWW is the acute pain point: nothing to do, everything to fear, symptom-spotting every hour.
2. *"I test way too early and way too often — how do I stop?"* — over-testing (multiple HPTs/day, testing at 7 DPO) that produces false negatives and fresh panic.
3. *"Is [twinge / cramp / sore boob / no symptoms] a sign?"* — symptom-spotting she knows is unreliable but can't stop (see §D: early-pregnancy and PMS symptoms are largely **indistinguishable**).
4. *"I've been Googling symptoms by DPO at 2am again."* — the research spiral; craving certainty the body can't give this early.
5. *"Everyone says 'just relax' — does stress actually stop me getting pregnant?"* — she needs the honest answer (**no clear causal evidence**) delivered as *relief*, not blame.
6. *"My period came again and I fell apart."* — the monthly let-down grief; each cycle-start is a small loss.
7. *"How do I track without the tracking making it worse?"* — wants *minimal, calming* tracking, not a data cockpit.
8. *"When is it 'too long' / when should I actually worry?"* — wants the honest 12-mo (<35) / 6-mo (≥35) evaluation guidance as *reassurance* ("you're not there yet, and it's a discussion, not a failure"), not a countdown to doom.
9. *"Is my anxiety normal, or is it a problem I should get help for?"* — wants a gentle, non-pathologizing way to check in on her mental health.
10. *(If prior loss)* *"This wait is worse because last time it ended badly."* — trauma-amplified TWW (→ shares surfaces with C5).

**Emotional arc — recurs every single cycle (design must respect the loop):**
- **Fertile window / "the trying":** hope + pressure; timing anxiety, "did we do it right?", performance stress.
- **The two-week wait (acute):** the hardest stretch — hypervigilance, symptom-spotting, urge to test early, catastrophizing. **This is where the app most helps or most harms.**
- **Testing:** compulsive early testing → false negatives → panic → re-test. Or the dreaded single line.
- **Result:**
  - *Period arrives:* let-down grief, "again," self-blame, dread of starting over.
  - *Positive test:* not clean joy but **fear** ("now I have something to lose") — especially with prior loss → anxiety-aware pregnancy (D2), never confetti.
- Then the loop **resets**, and the anticipatory dread of the next wait begins.

**Anti-needs / trust-breakers (what makes her more anxious, then drives churn):**
- **Obsessive countdowns & urgency banners** — "X days until test day," "CHANCE TODAY: HIGH," pulsing timers. These manufacture urgency and feed the spiral. (See §C.)
- **Bottomless symptom-by-DPO logging** that invites her to over-interpret noise as signal.
- **"Test now!" nudges** that encourage early testing (→ false negatives → panic).
- **Toxic positivity / stress-blame:** "just relax," "stop stressing and it'll happen," "your stress is the problem." Blames her for a thing she can't control and is **not evidence-based** (§D).
- **False certainty:** any prediction dressed as a promise; a confident "you're probably pregnant!" from symptom data.
- **Gamified streaks/engagement mechanics** that reward compulsive logging.
- **Privacy creep:** TTC + (possible) loss + mental-health-screener data is exceptionally sensitive; category leak history (Flo FTC 2021, Premom FTC 2023) makes mistrust rational.

**Feature requests recurring in reviews/forums:**
- A **"minimal mode"** / calm mode that tracks the basics and *hides* the anxiety-amplifying machinery.
- **No countdown** to test day — or the ability to fully turn timers off.
- **TWW coping tools** — grounding exercises, distraction prompts, "step away from the tests" gentle nudges.
- **Honest content** on symptom-spotting, when to test, and the stress question — that reassures without lying.
- A **mood check-in** that's caring, not clinical, with real support if she's struggling.
- Control to **mute** the app during the wait if she needs to.

## D. Clinical grounding (cited)

> All claims below are patient-education framing. Femio content is medically reviewed and cites primary sources. Femio does **not** diagnose, treat, prevent, or contracept; it educates and helps users cope and organize information for their clinician.

**When "trying" becomes a clinical conversation (threshold = routing, never a verdict):**
- Evaluation generally begins after **12 months** of regular unprotected intercourse for those **under 35**, after **6 months** for those **35 or older**, and more immediately over 40; evaluate without delay when a known infertility risk factor is present. This is a threshold for offering a clinician conversation, not a diagnosis, prognosis, or failure line; local referral pathways may differ. [ASRM *Fertility Evaluation of Infertile Women* (2021)]

**The two-week wait & symptom-spotting (the honest, anxiety-lowering truth):**
- Symptoms alone cannot confirm pregnancy because common early-pregnancy symptoms overlap with symptoms that can occur before a period. A pregnancy test—not symptom-spotting—is the appropriate way to check. [Office on Women’s Health, *Pregnancy tests*]

**Testing timing (to defuse over-testing / false negatives):**
- Home pregnancy tests detect **hCG**, which is produced after a fertilized egg attaches to the uterine wall. Testing too early can produce a **false negative** because hCG may still be below the test’s detection level. [MedlinePlus, *Pregnancy Test*]
- Follow the current test instructions. Testing after the first day of a missed period is generally more accurate; if the result is negative and pregnancy is still suspected or the period remains absent, repeat according to the label or contact a clinician. Femio does not publish a universal accuracy percentage or turn a DPO estimate into a guarantee. [Office on Women’s Health, *Pregnancy tests*; MedlinePlus]

**The stress–fertility question (must be delivered as relief, not blame):**
- Women trying to conceive — especially in fertility care — show **higher rates of anxiety and depression** than the general population; roughly **30–40%** of women in infertility populations report depressive mood or anxiety. Distress is *common and valid.* [PMC review; Fertility and Sterility]
- There is **no conclusive evidence that higher stress reduces the chance of having a baby from fertility treatment**. Femio does not overextend that evidence into a universal claim about every form of stress or natural conception, and it bans blame-based language such as “just relax” or “stress is stopping you.” [HFEA, *In vitro fertilisation (IVF)*, accessed 23 July 2026]

**What actually helps anxiety while TTC (evidence for the coping features):**
- **Mind–body / cognitive-behavioral programs** for people trying to conceive reduce anxiety and depressive symptoms in multiple randomized trials; a 2024 RCT found the **cognitive-behavioral + stress-management** elements drove significantly greater improvement in trait anxiety versus support alone. [Human Reproduction 2024 (Oxford); PMC]
- **Mindfulness, relaxation (deep breathing, progressive muscle relaxation), gentle movement/yoga, distraction, expressive writing/journaling, and naming feelings with a trusted person** are consistently recommended coping strategies for the TWW and TTC distress. [systematic review, Arch Womens Ment Health; clinician coping guides]
- **Routing, not treating:** guideline bodies recommend **screening for mood/anxiety and offering referral** to mental-health support; counselling/CBT via GP or accredited fertility counsellors is the appropriate path. Femio screens and *routes*. [ASRM; Fertility Network UK; Maternal Mental Health Alliance]

**Anxiety screening tool (non-diagnostic):**
- The **GAD-7** is a validated 7-item anxiety screener scored 0–21. The original study describes cut points of **5, 10, and 15** and identifies **10** as a reasonable threshold for further evaluation; Femio presents bands as screening, never diagnosis. **GAD-7/GAD-2 has no self-harm item:** a high total routes to clinician/support follow-up, while a separately disclosed self-harm concern uses the urgent route independently. Use only an authorized, validated, unmodified language version with attribution under [`docs/compliance/MENTAL_HEALTH_SCREENERS.md`](../compliance/MENTAL_HEALTH_SCREENERS.md). ([Spitzer et al., 2006](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamainternalmedicine/articlepdf/410326/ioi60000.pdf); NHS England NCCR accessed 23 July 2026; reviewed Mohamed K., 23 July 2026.)

*Femio applies all of the above as **educational reassurance, coping support, and information organization**, always ending "discuss with your clinician" — never as a diagnosis, treatment, prediction-as-promise, or "safe/fertile day" instruction.*

Medical-review resolution: fertility-evaluation, symptom, pregnancy-test, and stress wording follows [`FERTILITY_AWARENESS_SAFETY.md`](../compliance/FERTILITY_AWARENESS_SAFETY.md); GAD-7 handling follows [`MENTAL_HEALTH_SCREENERS.md`](../compliance/MENTAL_HEALTH_SCREENERS.md). *(Reviewed Mohamed K., 23 July 2026; next review 23 July 2027.)*

11. **Prior-loss bridge** — if she has a loss, seamless access to C5/TTC-after-loss surfaces (trauma-amplified TWW support) without forcing her fully into a loss persona.
12. **Partner-awareness content** — "your partner may worry differently, not less"; optional, consent-gated; solo users first-class.

## G. Tools, logs & alerts (compliant framing; anxiety-reducing by design)

**Design north star for C12:** every tool must be evaluated against one question — *does this lower or raise her anxiety?* If it raises it without a clear user-chosen benefit, it is off by default.

**Logs (inputs — minimal by default, depth is opt-in):**
- Period {start, end, flow}, optional single fertile-window awareness input, optional (off-by-default) symptom/mood check-in, optional test log (with over-testing guardrail), optional OPK/BBT **only if she turns on depth**, coping-tool usage (private), mood/anxiety screener results {tool, score, date, non-diagnostic, routed}. If prior loss: loss records (shared with C5 schema).

**Tools:**
- Calm-Mode controller (the minimal/depth toggle), Two-Week-Wait companion (coping-first), grounding/breathing exercises, expressive-writing space, honest-reassurance content cards, over-testing gentle guardrail, non-diagnostic mood screener + crisis-resource router, cycle-start gentle handler, mute/take-a-break control, anxiety-aware positive-test path (→ D2), prior-loss bridge (→ C5).

**THE ANXIETY-REDUCING DESIGN CONTRACT (non-negotiable):**
1. **Minimal by default.** New C12 users land in Calm Mode: period + optional gentle fertile-window awareness only. Deep symptom/OPK/BBT logging is **opt-in**, never the default, never gamified.
2. **No obsessive countdown.** No pulsing "days until test," no "CHANCE TODAY: HIGH," no urgency banners. Fertile-window and any test-date reminder are **calm, static, and singular**; a test-date reminder is **opt-in and fires once**, gently.
3. **The TWW is a support surface, not a countdown.** During the wait the home foregrounds **coping tools**, not symptom-logging or a timer. Symptom logging in the TWW is available but never pushed, and is paired with "symptoms can't tell you yet" context.
4. **Over-testing is met with kindness, never scolding or restriction.** Frequent/early test logging → one gentle, evidence-based note about false negatives + reliable timing. She can always test; Femio just refuses to *urge* it.
5. **No engagement mechanics that reward compulsion.** No streaks, no logging-completion gamification, no re-engagement nudges built on urgency. Retention is earned by calm, not anxiety.
6. **Mood screening is caring, not clinical-cold, and always routes on risk.** Framed as a check-in; **any high GAD score or self-harm indicator → immediate localized crisis resources**, regardless of total. Screening ≠ diagnosis.
7. **Fail-safe to calm.** If state is ambiguous (e.g., possible loss, unclear result), default to the **gentlest** surface and **suppress** any celebratory/urgent nudge. Silence-over-anxiety.

**Alerts / nudges (all opt-in, calm-toned, revocable):**
- Test-date (opt-in, single): "Whenever you're ready — a test is most reliable from your missed period onward. No rush." — never "TEST TODAY!"
- TWW check-in (opt-in): "The wait can be a lot. Want a grounding exercise, or just to note how you're doing?" — never a symptom demand.
- Cycle-start (gentle): if her period arrives — a soft acknowledgment, **not** "new cycle, let's try again!"; opt-in coping content.
- Over-testing guardrail (kind, rare): only if frequent/early testing is logged; one gentle note, easily dismissed.
- Mood check-in (periodic, opt-in): warm, non-pathologizing; results always paired with support options.

**Mandatory compliance wording on relevant surfaces:**
- *"Femio is for information and tracking. We don't diagnose, and we can't tell you if you're pregnant — a test or your clinician can."*
- Screener outputs: *"This is a screening check-in, not a diagnosis. Please discuss results with a clinician."* + crisis resources on high scores.
- Fertile-window surfaces: *"An estimate based on your data — not a guarantee, and not a 'safe day' tool."*
- No "safe/infertile day" language — **ever**.

## I. Personalization rules (wizard → home config; anxiety-reducing; hidden-but-reachable)

**Onboarding wizard (branch into C12) — trauma-informed, minimal, skippable:**
1. "What brings you to Femio?" → *I'm trying to conceive.* → Q3 refine.
2. Gentle, **optional**: "How are you feeling about it?" offered plainly (e.g., *excited / anxious / a mix / it's been hard*). Selecting anxiety/"it's been hard" sets `anxiety_context` and **enters Calm Mode by default**. No forced detail.
3. "Have you experienced a pregnancy loss?" (optional, no euphemism, skippable) → if yes, sets `prior_loss` and offers C5/TTC-after-loss bridge.
4. "How would you like to track?" → **defaults to "Keep it simple / minimal"**, with "I like detail" available — sets `tracking_intensity`. Framed so *minimal is the recommended, normal choice*, not a lesser one.
5. "When your period comes, how should Femio handle it?" → acknowledge gently / stay quiet / hide → `cycle_start_grief_pref`.
6. "Would a gentle mental-health check-in be helpful?" (opt-in) → enables the non-diagnostic screener.
7. "Involve a partner?" or *I'm doing this on my own* → equal-weight solo path.
8. Consent & privacy choices (heightened sensitivity notice — mental-health + TTC data).

**Home config for C12 (varies by state):**
- **Calm Mode (default):** minimal tracker + one calm fertile-window awareness card + honest-reassurance content + easy access to coping tools. **Zero** countdowns, urgency banners, or symptom checklists pushed.
- **Two-week wait (active):** hero = **TWW coping companion** (grounding, distraction, "how are you really"); symptom logging demoted and context-wrapped; **no timer.**
- **Cycle-start (period arrived):** soft, validating surface per her `cycle_start_grief_pref`; opt-in coping; **no "let's try again!" cheer.**
- **Elevated anxiety (screener/self-report):** mental-health module + resources foregrounded; coping tools pinned; support routing surfaced.
- **Positive test:** → anxiety-aware pregnancy (D2) handoff; **no confetti**; "however you're feeling is okay."
- **Prior loss:** blends C5 gentleness over C12 (trauma-amplified TWW); most-tender tone wins.
- **Hidden-but-reachable:** full lifespan library one tap away — but nothing anxiety-amplifying is ever *pushed* in Calm/TWW states.

**Adaptive tone (the tightrope):** always **validate-and-inform, lower the temperature.** Never "just relax," "stop stressing," "it'll happen when you least expect it," or doom. When surfacing facts, lead with the *reassuring, guilt-lifting* framing (symptoms can't tell you → permission to stop; stress isn't your fault → relief; most conceive within a year → hope), all sourced.

## J. Adaptation triggers (state transitions)

*C12 inherits all universal events from the Master Adaptation Model §6 (positive test, pregnancy loss, mood-screen-high/crisis, red-flag symptom, disengagement/break, toggle-off, age-of-consent). The table below specifies only C12-specific transitions and how C12 tunes the universals.*

| Trigger | Detection | Femio response | Routes to |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Enters two-week wait** | ovulation estimate / user marks | Switch home to **coping-first TWW companion**; foreground grounding/distraction; demote & context-wrap symptom logging; **no countdown, no timer.** | TWW companion (Calm) |
| **Over-testing detected** | frequent/early `test_log[]` entries | **Kind, one-time** guardrail note (false negatives + reliable timing); never scold, never restrict. | Educational nudge (stay) |
| **Period arrives (let-down)** | `period_start` logged during TTC | Apply `cycle_start_grief_pref`: gentle acknowledgment / quiet / hide; **no "new cycle!" cheer**; offer opt-in coping. | Cycle-start gentle handling |
| **Escalating distress / anxiety-screen flag** | GAD-7 ≥10, GAD-2 elevated, or self-reported distress; a self-harm concern is captured separately because GAD instruments contain no self-harm item | Validate warmly; a high anxiety score routes to clinician/support follow-up. A separately disclosed self-harm concern triggers the global urgent route independently of the anxiety total. **Never diagnose.** | **Mental-health routing** (urgent path only for a separate self-harm/immediate-danger signal) |
| **Prior loss present / disclosed** | `prior_loss:true` | Blend **C5 gentleness** over C12: trauma-amplified TWW support, loss-aware tone, C5 surfaces reachable; most-tender tone wins. | **C5 / TTC-after-loss bridge** |
| **Positive pregnancy test** | HPT positive / user confirms | *(Tunes universal positive-test handler)* **Do NOT celebrate loudly / no confetti.** Suppress fertile-window/TWW surfaces. Offer switch to **anxiety-aware Pregnancy (D2)**: milestone-paced, honest, "fear now is normal, however you feel is okay"; carry anxiety_context + any loss history forward. | **D2 — anxiety-aware Pregnancy** (D2 tone even without prior loss, given high anxiety) |
| **Requests mute / take-a-break** | taps "pause" / inactivity | Pause **all** TTC nudges and surfaces; keep data; quiet by default; easy return. | Mute state (retain, silent) |
| **12-mo (<35) / 6-mo (≥35) reached, no conception** | `ttc_start_date` elapsed | Non-diagnostic, **reassurance-framed** education: "many couples conceive within a year; a check-in with a clinician is reasonable now — not a failure." Offer, never force. | Fertility-conversation education (optional → C-cohort evaluation personas) |
| **Loss during/after TTC** | user reports loss | *(Inherits universal loss handler)* Instant suppression + Gentle Mode + grief routing (→ C5/E6). | C5 / grief routing |

*All routes preserve data continuity (one account), default to **calm/silence over anxiety**, and always end with clinician-referral language — never a diagnosis, prediction-as-promise, or "safe day" instruction.*

## K. Compliance flags

1. **Anxiety-reducing design is an internal product-safety requirement.** Femio does not deploy urgency countdowns, unsolicited “test now” nudges, gamified logging, or engagement mechanics that exploit TTC anxiety. Minimal-by-default tracking and the §G anxiety-reducing contract are release criteria. This is a Femio design constraint, not an external clinical-effectiveness claim. *(Approved Mohamed K., 24 July 2026; next review 24 July 2027.)*
2. **No diagnosis / treatment / prevention / contraception.** Femio never diagnoses infertility or anxiety, never predicts conception as a promise, never gives "safe/fertile day" contraceptive framing, and never advises medication. Thresholds (12-mo/6-mo) and screeners (GAD-7) **educate & route only.**
3. **Mental-health screener handling.** GAD-7/GAD-2 is non-diagnostic screening; results receive elevated mental-health-data handling. A high score routes to clinician/support follow-up. Because GAD-7/GAD-2 contains no self-harm item, Femio must not infer self-harm from its total; a separately disclosed self-harm concern follows the urgent route. Use only an authorized, validated, unmodified language version under [`docs/compliance/MENTAL_HEALTH_SCREENERS.md`](../compliance/MENTAL_HEALTH_SCREENERS.md), with crisis routing from the global policy. *(Reviewed Mohamed K., 23 July 2026.)*
4. **Tone safety — no toxic positivity, no stress-blame, no doom.** Content policy bans "just relax," "stop stressing," "it'll happen when you stop trying," "your stress is the problem," and worst-case doom-framing. The stress-fertility statement must be **reassuring and accurate** (no clear causal evidence) — reviewed for emotional safety, not just accuracy.
5. **Uncertainty honesty.** Fertile-window and any timing surfaces are estimates with confidence, never promises; symptom data never yields "you're probably pregnant." No false certainty in either direction.
6. **Privacy-by-design / exceptional data sensitivity.** TTC, mental-health, and loss data are never sent to ad networks or used for targeted advertising. Apply minimum collection, granular/revocable product permissions, purpose-limited processors, and strict retention, export, deletion, and security controls under [`HEALTH_APP_CLAIMS_AND_DATA_SAFETY.md`](../compliance/HEALTH_APP_CLAIMS_AND_DATA_SAFETY.md). Primary warnings: [FTC Flo](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings/192-3133-flo-health-inc) and [FTC Premom](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings/202-3186-easy-healthcare-corporation-us-v). *(Reviewed Mohamed K., 24 July 2026; next review 24 July 2027.)*
7. **Privacy-law implementation.** For EEA processing, fertility and mental-health data fall within GDPR Article 9; document an Article 6 basis and Article 9 condition, use granular product permissions, and implement applicable data-subject rights. Other markets require launch-country counsel review. Do not claim universal “GDPR/PDPL compliance” or residency without approved architecture/contracts. ([GDPR official text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj); [`HEALTH_APP_CLAIMS_AND_DATA_SAFETY.md`](../compliance/HEALTH_APP_CLAIMS_AND_DATA_SAFETY.md)) *(Reviewed Mohamed K., 24 July 2026; next review 24 July 2027.)*
8. **Partner mode consent + solo parity.** Partner sees only granted scope; consent logged, revocable; solo users first-class, never nudged toward a partner.
9. **Medical review + citations.** Fertility-evaluation, symptom, pregnancy-test, stress, and screening wording is pinned in [`FERTILITY_AWARENESS_SAFETY.md`](../compliance/FERTILITY_AWARENESS_SAFETY.md) and [`MENTAL_HEALTH_SCREENERS.md`](../compliance/MENTAL_HEALTH_SCREENERS.md). Translation must preserve the meaning and be locale-QA’d before publication. *(Reviewed Mohamed K., 23 July 2026; next review 23 July 2027.)*
10. **App-store health-app policy.** Apply [`HEALTH_APP_CLAIMS_AND_DATA_SAFETY.md`](../compliance/HEALTH_APP_CLAIMS_AND_DATA_SAFETY.md): no fertility-boost, diagnosis, therapy, or anxiety-cure claim; substantiate wellbeing claims; provide required disclosures and clinician prompts; complete Google’s health-app declaration. Re-check both store policies immediately before submission. *(Reviewed Mohamed K., 24 July 2026; next review 24 July 2027.)*
